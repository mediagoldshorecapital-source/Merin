A complete messaging and growth system for **Prime Ingredients**, built from the
*NMN Deep Customer Intelligence Report* (20 May 2026) and cross-checked against
the Triple Whale warehouse through 7 August 2026.

## The headline correction

The research names **"Feeling more tired as you age? It's not just aging"** as the
hero hook. Across six months and 436 ads that exact construction returned
**0.92 ROAS, 0 winners, 9 losers.**

The committed version of the same insight — *"Tired of feeling tired as you age?
It's not just aging… Your NAD+ levels are plummeting!"* — returned **1.41** across
$146,474 and produced 4 of 12 all-time winners.

Same insight, same mechanism, same audience. **53% apart because one commits and
one hedges.** Keep the insight; rewrite every execution.

## Two further corrections (research is dated 20 May)

| Research says | Current data (1 May – 7 Aug, spend-controlled) |
| --- | --- |
| Saturday highest, Monday lowest by 22%, "never launch Monday" | **Friday best** (1.25 ROAS, $48.47 CPA); **Tuesday worst** (0.99). Saturday mid-pack, Monday fine. |
| "11 AM is your single best hour" | **7 PM (296) and 6 PM (291)** now peak; 11 AM is 5th. Curve is flat 8 AM–midnight; only 2–7 AM is dead. |

Raw order counts mislead on day-of-week because they track budget pacing, not
behaviour — Sunday looks big only because it is the largest spend day, and it
returns 1.05.

## Highest-value untested creative

"Not all X is created equal" is the best structure by CPM ($57.47 vs $98.37) and
produced 3 of 12 winners — but has **only ever run on Astaxanthin (~4% of
revenue)**, never on NMN (94%). It also directly answers her strongest objection
("I've been scammed before").

## The $1M question — the honest answer

Current run rate ~$118K/month.

- **$1M over 6 months** needs $167K/month (+41%) — realistic
- **$1M over 3 months** needs $333K/month (+183%) — only buyable with spend, and
  at 1.43 ROAS against a ~1.70 breakeven that destroys the margin funding
  everything else

Modelled at flat acquisition (877 new subs/month), 65% first-cycle and 80%
subsequent retention, the recurring base alone compounds to ~$936K over six
months. **Growth comes from the recurring block, not the acquisition block** —
you don't need more traffic, you need the base to compound and the average order
to get bigger.

Five levers, ranked by effort-to-return:

| Lever | Monthly impact | Effort |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1. AOV / bundle ladder (restore 6-bottle default) | +$25–40K | Low |
| 2. Email / Klaviyo (currently $1,028/mo) | +$25–35K | Low |
| 3. Stop discounting re-bills (37.9% → 15–20%) | +$6.7K margin | Very low |
| 4. Robert campaign | +$10–20K | Medium |
| 5. Branded search (5.08% CVR, 0.4% of orders) | +$5–15K | Medium |

## Blind spots flagged

1. Subscription churn is unmeasured — the whole model rests on it, with 2 months
   of data
2. No creative-level retention tracking (CAC-to-second-order by ad)
3. No creative mentions the subscription at all
4. Three of four products have null COGS
5. Single channel / product / country concentration
6. April data (~$90–100K) still not backfilled

## Caveats

Ad figures are Meta channel-reported — valid for ranking creative against
creative, not as absolute profit measures, since TW pixel attribution doesn't
exist before June 2026. Robert's 1.81 ROAS is carried from the research and is
not independently verifiable in the warehouse. Revenue projections are a model,
not a forecast.

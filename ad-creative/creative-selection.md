## 4. ⚠️ Compliance — fix before you upload

| Risk | Ads affected | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta Personal Attributes policy.** Second-person copy that implies you know the viewer's health state ("*You* already know how this goes", "*Your* doctor said it's normal") is a standing rejection trigger. | A01, A04, A06, A11, A13, A15 | Rewrite hooks to first-person creator narration: "I used to crash at 3 PM" instead of "You crash at 3 PM." Hook B on most of these ads is already first-person — **lead with Hook B.** |
| **Cognitive / memory adjacency.** Word-finding and mental-blank framing sits close to implied cognitive-decline diagnosis. | A06, A09, A12, A19 | Keep it to observable daily wins ("the word arrived"), never "memory," "decline," or "fog" as a condition. |
| **NMN regulatory status.** FDA's position excludes NMN from the dietary-supplement definition; some ad platforms reject on ingredient name alone. | All | Confirm ad-account standing before a large upload. Have the COA and 30-day guarantee page live at the destination URL. |
| **Unsourced statistic:** "64% of what's sold was never tested." | A21 | Cite the source on-screen or cut the number. This is your best-performing ad — don't lose it to a substantiation review. |
| **Dose claim:** "1,000mg clinical serving," "matches the human trials." | A13, A23, A25, A30 | Have the referenced trials on file. |

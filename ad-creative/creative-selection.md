# Prime Ingredients — Creative Selection Worksheet

**Source:** `50_ads_with_12_frameworks.docx` · **45 scripts read** (A01–A45) · **12 frameworks**
**Core set:** 31 assets · **Reserve:** 14 · **Distinct angles:** 8

Every ad in the file was read and scored on four criteria: how hard the hook lands in the
first three seconds, how much verifiable proof it carries, whether it makes an argument no
other ad in the set makes, and whether the format suits the message.

---

## 1. Three things the file doesn't tell you

These change what you can promise, so they come before the selection.

| Flag | Finding | Action |

## 5. Two casting and inventory gaps

**Creator concentration.** "Linda" carries 18 of the 45 scripts. If one creator shoots all of
them you get fatigue on a single face and one point of failure. Split the Linda ads across two
or three different women — that also gets the "different creators" box ticked honestly.

**No review-screenshot static.** There is no true five-star-review static in the document; A05
substitutes by using the community's own verbatim metaphor. Build one anyway — it's the
cheapest asset in the whole mix and routinely lands top three.

## 7. Compliance — five flags to fix before upload

These are rejection triggers, not stylistic notes. Four of them are fixed by swapping which
hook variation you lead with — the scripts already contain the safer version.

| Risk | Affected | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal-attributes policy.** Second-person copy implying you know the viewer's health state — "*You* already know how this goes", "*Your* doctor said it's normal". | A01 · A04 · A06 · A11 · A13 · A15 | Rewrite to first-person creator narration: "I used to crash at 3 PM," not "You crash at 3 PM." **Hook B on most of these ads is already first-person — lead with Hook B.** |
| **Cognitive adjacency.** Word-finding and mental-blank framing sits close to an implied cognitive-decline diagnosis. | A06 · A09 · A12 · A19 | Keep it to observable daily wins — "the word arrived" — and never use *memory*, *decline* or *fog* as a named condition. |
| **Ingredient status.** FDA's position excludes NMN from the dietary-supplement definition; some ad accounts reject on the ingredient name alone. | All 45 | Confirm account standing before a bulk upload, and have the COA and 30-day guarantee live on the destination page first. |
| **Unsourced statistic:** "64% of what's sold was never tested." | A21 | Cite the source on-screen or cut the number. This is your best-performing angle — don't lose it to a substantiation review. |
| **Dose claims:** "1,000mg clinical serving," "the numbers that match the human trials." | A13 · A23 · A25 · A30 | Have the referenced trials on file and linked before these run. |

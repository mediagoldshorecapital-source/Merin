                Fix before upload

                Five compliance flags that will cost you ads

                These are rejection triggers, not stylistic notes. Four of them are fixed by swapping which hook variation you lead with — the scripts already contain the safer version.

                RiskAffectedFix
                Personal-attributes policy. Second-person copy implying you know the viewer's health state — "You already know how this goes", "Your doctor said it's normal". A01 · A04 · A06 · A11 · A13 · A15 Rewrite to first-person creator narration: "I used to crash at 3 PM," not "You crash at 3 PM." Hook B on most of these ads is already first-person — lead with Hook B.
                Cognitive adjacency. Word-finding and mental-blank framing sits close to an implied cognitive-decline diagnosis. A06 · A09 · A12 · A19 Keep it to observable daily wins — "the word arrived" — and never use memory, decline or fog as a named condition.
                Ingredient status. FDA's position excludes NMN from the dietary-supplement definition; some ad accounts reject on the ingredient name alone. All 45 Confirm account standing before a bulk upload, and have the COA and 30-day guarantee live on the destination page first.
                Unsourced statistic. "64% of what's sold was never tested." A21 Cite the source on-screen or cut the number. This is your best-performing angle — don't lose it to a substantiation review.
                Dose claims. "1,000mg clinical serving," "the numbers that match the human trials." A13 · A23 · A25 · A30 Have the referenced trials on file and linked before these run.

# KNOWLEDGE 01 · The 16 Creative Frameworks

Twelve from *Scripting Frameworks 2026*, four from *Ads Framework 1–4*. Each entry gives the beat structure,
the fill-in formula, and — the part that matters — **the Prime adaptation**, because every one of these
frameworks was written for a category where you may assert things about the reader. Prime is not that category.

> **The one adaptation all 16 require.** Every beat that asserts something about her must be rewritten as a
> question she answers internally, or a disclosure the speaker makes about himself. *"You've started saying
> maybe later"* is a behaviour she recognises. *"Your NAD+ is collapsing"* is a diagnosis. Same insight, one is
> shippable. This is Law 4 and it overrides every template below.

## Framework 1 · Bandwagon Effect — **C.R.O.W.D.**

*Origin: 250310_BIOGHK (Soothe). Target: Unaware → Problem-Aware.*

| Beat | Goal | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **C** — Call out the movement | Create curiosity by naming what relevant groups, experts or authorities are doing | *"Why do [expert group] swear by [thing] over [common thing]?"* |
| **R** — Reject the old way | Educate on why the common solution is weaker than they think | *"Most people spend thousands on [old solution], but [truth]."* |
| **O** — Onboard the new way | Introduce your product as the superior solution | *"And [brand] puts [mechanism] into [format], so it [action] at [level]."* |
| **W** — Wave the proof | Social proof, studies, case studies | *"This isn't another hyped ingredient — it's backed by [named, citable evidence]."* |
| **D** — Direct the viewer | CTA that restates the main benefit | *"Get it at [brand] now, to [core benefit]."* |

**Prime adaptation ⚠**
- **Do not name David Sinclair.** Net negative in this corpus. Do not use a celebrity — G5 shows women in the exact target band reacting with personal offence (*"Because no one wants to be like the Kardashians. Be a Beth!"*).
- The safe "movement" for Prime is **peer-shaped, not expert-shaped**: women 58–68 who stopped calling it normal. Or the informed-buyer movement: people who read the panel before the headline.
- The `W` beat must carry a citation you can put **on screen**. No unsourced "64% fail label claim" and no "NAD+ drops 50% by middle age" — both are contested inside the corpus itself.
- Best Prime use: the **"first food"** angle — NMN occurs naturally in human breast milk, *"the very first foods humans eat after they are born."* Same emotional payload as the conspiracy angle with none of the exposure.

## Framework 2 · Industry Contrarian — **D.I.S.R.U.P.T.**

*Origin: Epic Ads Lab / Soothe. Target: Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware.*

| Beat | Goal |
|---|---|
| **D** — Declassify the secret | Expose suppressed knowledge or debunk a myth |
| **I** — Industry failure | Explain why mainstream solutions fail |
| **S** — Solution | Introduce yours |
| **R** — Reinforce with evidence | Studies, case studies, real examples |
| **U** — Upstage the competition | What makes yours unique |
| **P** — Provide proof | Measurable real-world adoption |
| **T** — Trigger the movement | CTA |

**Prime adaptation**
- This is the natural home of **Territory 24 — "the study that didn't go our way."** The 2025 systematic review will be quoted at you regardless. Raise it yourself, then make the endpoint-mismatch argument the community already upvoted. Being the brand that raises its own inconvenient evidence is worth more than any claim you could make instead.
- Also the home of **Territory 09 — the creatine argument.** The highest-scoring objection in the corpus is a cancer researcher saying NAD+ is *"just an electron carrier, so supplementation won't help."* A commenter replied: creatine is just a phosphate carrier too, and it obviously works. The researcher never answered. That is Prime's single best owned argument, and it is borrowed from the audience.
- **Never** run the FDA-conspiracy version (*"the supplement they tried to ban"*). It sells, and it recruits exactly the customers who churn fastest while raising the regulatory profile.
- `D` must declassify a **fact**, not a conspiracy. "Most of what's sold was never tested by anyone but the seller" is defensible and lands harder than a borrowed statistic.

## Framework 3 · Listicle — **C.U.R.E.**

*Origin: Vitauthority. Target: Problem-Aware. Strong for AI VO + static-heavy edits.*

| Beat | Goal | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **C** — Curiosity (list hook) | Spark intrigue, **then** give the reason for the number | Bad: *"5 hidden mistakes making your sleep worse."* Good: *"If you had 8h sleep but still wake up tired — here are 5 mistakes making it worse."* |
| **U** — Uncover failures | Expose why the standard approaches fail, one per item | Name the specific failed fix, not the category |
| **R** — Remedy | Introduce the product as the answer | One product, one mechanism |
| **E** — Evidence | Credibility / social proof | Must be citable |
| **E** — Engage | CTA with risk reversal | 30-day guarantee, above the fold |

**Prime adaptation**
- The `U` beats write themselves from the **eleven failed solutions** in the language bank: more sleep, earlier nights, naps, more coffee, B12, the multivitamin, cutting sugar, the doctor saying bloodwork is normal.
- The strongest Prime listicle spine: **"3 reasons more sleep isn't fixing your mornings — and it's not your age."** It renames the problem (Problem-Aware's whole job) instead of promising a result.
- Keep the count to **3**, not 5. 20-second Reels cannot carry five beats plus a remedy and hold the timestamp discipline.
- The `C` hook must qualify. *"If you sleep eight hours and still wake up tired"* excludes everyone who doesn't. That is the point.

## Framework 4 · Founder — **F.O.U.N.D.E.**

*Origin: 250217_FOUNDERWC (Community Wolf). Target: Unaware → Product-Aware. Highest trust-per-second format available.*

| Beat | Goal | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **F** — Feature the founder | Introduce a relatable human behind the mission | *"This is [name], and he's a regular guy."* |
| **O** — Opposing force | The enemy/problem the buyer faces | *"[Scale of the problem, one number]."* |
| **U** — Unlocking the idea | The breakthrough moment, framed as an intuitive pivot | *"So we thought — what if…"* |
| **N** — Numbers | 3–4 tangible outcomes: numbers, timelines | *"In just [time], we've [3 results]."* |
| **D** — Destiny | Current wins as the beginning of a bigger mission | *"And we're just getting started."* |
| **E** — Engage the movement | CTA using "join" language, identity shift | *"Join the movement / comment [word]."* |

**Prime adaptation — this is a P0 format for Prime, for one specific reason**
- The word **"scam" is already in Prime's comment threads under paid impressions.** The brand will be judged on that word whether or not it says it. Saying it first, and answering it with a document, converts the largest liability into the most defensible asset.
- `N` (Numbers) for Prime is **spec, not growth**: 1000mg per serving · 2 capsules · lot number on the bottle · COA for that lot · no proprietary blend. Not revenue, not customer counts, not "5,128 reviews" (the review platform returns zero rows — do not cite a number you cannot show).
- `D` (Destiny) must not become a mission-statement swell. Her register is grief and anger, not inspiration. Land it flat.
- 🔒 The fulfilment beat (*"some of you have waited too long"*) **may not be shot until the fulfilment SLA and a staffed phone line are both real.** Saying it before it is true converts one liability into two.

## Framework 5 · How You Can X Without Y — **S.I.M.P.L.E.**

*Origin: 241219_DADBB (Three Beacon). Target: Problem-Aware. Best identity-qualifying opener in the set.*

| Beat | Goal | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **S** — State the escape | Identity + pain in one line | *"How to [desire] without [hated friction]"* |
| **I** — Identify pain | Mirror the specific frustrating scenario | Name the scene, not the feeling |
| **M** — Minimal solution | The "1-thing fix" — the only thing you need | *"Our [product] replaces [N] things"* |
| **P** — Prove | Feature → benefit with because/so logic | *"[Feature] → [benefit] without [side effect]"* |
| **L** — Lifestyle win | Contrast past effort with a low-effort outcome | *"No steps. You just [action] and you're done."* |
| **E** — Escape CTA | Identity-tailored minimalism | *"If you're [identity] who [constraint] → [action]"* |

**Prime adaptation**
- This is the natural container for **Territory 11 — "One capsule. Not eleven."** The corpus is full of people taking a dozen supplements who admit they have no idea which one is doing anything. Simplicity as a *diagnostic* advantage, not a convenience one.
- ⚠ **Guardrail: "simple" must never read as "underdosed."** This market punishes thin formulas harder than complex ones — a Researcher-tier reviewer will point out that NMN consumes methyl groups. **Make simplicity a claim about the routine, never about the formula.**
- The `S` line for Prime: *"How to stop losing your afternoons — without adding another thing to the pile."*
- Also the best container for the **untested retention angle**: 97% of orders are subscriptions and recurring charges are the only profitable order type, yet not one ad in six months mentions replenishment or "never run out." `S` = *"How to never run out — without a subscription you can't cancel."* 🔒 blocked on G3 until a visible one-time option and a self-serve cancel link are genuinely live.

## Framework 6 · Organic — **P.U.R.E.**

*Origin: 250313_WORLDGTD (Alleyoop). Target: Unaware. The most native-feeling format on TikTok/Reels.*

| Beat | Goal | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **P** — Problem uncovered | Hook with raw, relatable, blunt frustration | *"[Blunt statement of the failure] — until this."* |
| **U** — Unfiltered chaos | 2–3 scenarios showing the benefit living in a real day | Timestamped: *"At 7am… by 10am… at 4pm…"* |
| **R** — Real evidence | An organic "aha" captured in a natural environment | The discovery, not the demo |
| **E** — Effortless freedom | The anti-hassle payoff, contrasted against the old struggle | Short. One line. |

**Prime adaptation — the single best structural fit for Linda**
- The `U` beat's timestamped-day structure is **exactly** the register the corpus rewards. Her whole symptom set is time-stamped: 6:30 AM unrested · 7 AM the stack of six things · 2–4 PM the crash · 6–7 PM the recliner. **Name the hour, not the feeling.**
- `R` maps directly onto **Proof Tier 3 — involuntary discovery** and **Tier 4 — the stop-start experiment**, the two most persuasive shapes available. *"I didn't buy it for that."* / *"I notice when I don't take it."*
- Keep the register **flat and faintly irritated** — telling you a fact about her day she has stopped questioning. Not sad, not warm, not selling.
- Available light, visible clutter, phone-shot. Plain beats polished: in this account winners look homemade and losers look like adverts.

## Framework 7 · **P.A.S.** — Problem · Agitate · Solution

*Origin: 250401_ANXMLB (Three Beacon). Target: Problem-Aware. The workhorse.*

| Beat | Goal | Devices |
|---|---|---|
| **P** — Problem | Address the core pain in her words | Emotional vulnerability + the failed solution that backfired |
| **A** — Agitate | Deepen it emotionally, not just physically | Physical suffering · hopelessness · isolation · loss aversion |
| **S** — Solution | Unique mechanism + relief + social backing | *"Unlike [basic approach], [product] [specific mechanism]"* |

**Prime adaptation**
- The `A` beat is where scripts go wrong. **Agitate the injustice, not the decline.** Her tell is *"I should not feel like this"* — not "I'm getting old." She hasn't accepted this, which is exactly why she's still shopping. Agitating decline makes her defensive; agitating unfairness makes her lean in.
- The failed-solution device is strong here and claim-free: *"A doctor once told me it was normal. Normal isn't the same as fine."*
- **Do not agitate toward a diagnosis.** The witnessed blank, not dementia. The lie-down after work, not chronic fatigue. (Law 3.)
- `S` must not promise speed. *"It's not caffeine. Nothing will happen on day one."* — that line filters refund-seekers, sets a survivable horizon, and reads as honesty in a category built on instant-miracle promises.

## Framework 8 · **U.G.L.Y.** Ads — Unpolished · Gritty · Lean · Yelling-for-action

*Origin: 250103_PAPER (Switch Research). Not a beat structure — a treatment you apply to any structure (PAS, AIDA, CURE).*

Apply three questions to whatever script you already have:

**1 · "How can I make this SIMPLER?"**
Can I say this in five words or less? What 70% of the words can I delete? Would a ten-year-old understand it?
→ Replace sentences with bullet text cards.

**2 · "How can I show this CHEAPLY?"**
What can I film with a $0 budget? How would a bored intern present this? Which free-app effect looks tacky but eye-catching?
→ Vertical phone camera, natural light, shadows welcome. Crude arrows, neon text, paper-tear hooks.

**3 · "What's the OPPOSITE of competitors?"**
What would make a creative director cry? Which industry rule can I violently break? What truth is everyone avoiding?
→ Swap beauty shots for unflattering reality. Use negative emotion instead of joy.

**Prime adaptation — this is measured truth here, not a stylistic gamble**
- **In this account winners look homemade and losers look like adverts.** [M] The best single ad in six months has a 3.04% CTR — roughly half the account average — and converts 5.57% of the clicks it does buy.
- Question 3 for Prime has an obvious answer: **every competitor shows a glowing 40-year-old.** The opposite is a 64-year-old woman sitting on the edge of the bed at 6:31 AM, not moving. Or a man's hand gripping the truck bed as he steps down.
- ⚠ **Where UGLY stops:** it does not licence disease language, uncited numbers, a competitor's bottle in frame, or a fabricated document. Ugly production, clean claims.
- ⚠ **No "before/after" on the Mirror pillar even in ugly treatment.** Ever.
- Cheapest high-volume test format in the set. Use it to run the length ladder and the hook swap at near-zero cost.

## Framework 9 · Founder Objections — **P.R.O.V.E.**

*Origin: 250217_IMPOSSWC (Community Wolf). Target: Product-Aware / Most-Aware. Lowest CPA rung on the ladder.*

| Beat | Goal | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **P** — Problem (stark reality hook) | Lead with an undeniable painful truth | *"They said [X] was impossible."* / *"Somebody wrote 'scam' under one of our ads on Tuesday."* |
| **R** — Reframe (founder credibility) | Contrast critics with your identity | *"But we're [identity]."* |
| **O** — Objection (name the doubt) | Voice the specific skepticism out loud | *"Everyone said [objection]."* |
| **V** — Victory (proof) | Crush the doubt with something tangible | The document, on camera |
| **E** — Expand (bigger vision) | Pivot to the larger ambition | Keep it flat for Prime |

**Prime adaptation — the highest-leverage framework in the set for this account right now**
- Prime has **seven named public allegations** sitting under live ads. P.R.O.V.E. is the only framework built to walk straight at them. **One gap per asset** — never a portmanteau that answers the easy ones and skips the hard one.
- `O` must name the objection **in the customer's own words**, verbatim: *"You show MicroIngredients bottle in your ad, but sell a different brand."* *"It says 120 in the bottle but mine only had 30."* *"I wanna order but I don't want a subscription."*
- `V` must be **a physical object held to lens**, not a sentence: the purple bottle held steady for ten seconds with the label legible · the lot number under rack focus · the COA sheet with that lot circled in pen · the capsules counted on white.
- 🔒 Each variant is blocked on its own gate. Write them all now; upload only the ones whose gate has closed.
- `E` for Prime is not a mission swell. It is a standing offer: *"Send it to any lab you like. If the assay doesn't match the certificate, we refund the order and pay for the test."* ⚠ needs written terms live on site before it ships.

## Framework 10 · Us vs Them — **S.H.O.W.**

*Origin: 250303_CHALLETS (Elite Tactical Systems). Target: Solution-Aware. Demonstration format.*

| Beat | Goal | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **S** — Set the challenge | Pose a high-stakes, everyday challenge | *"Can I [task] before [everyday timer]?"* |
| **H** — Head-to-head showdown | Split-screen, both actions simultaneously | Visual, not narrated |
| **O** — Outcome & why | Reveal the winner, then explain the feature that won it | Outcome first, mechanism second |
| **W** — Win & CTA | If/then logic linking the outcome to the viewer's payoff | *"If [outcome] sounds good → [action]"* |

**Prime adaptation ⚠ — the tightest constraint of any framework here**
- **Comparative is allowed on SPECIFICATION only, never on efficacy.** Dose, form, purity, format, cost per gram. You may show *"1000mg vs 250mg — four of theirs to match two of ours."* You may **not** show or imply "ours works and theirs doesn't."
- Never name or show a competitor's bottle while G2 is open. Use a **generic unbranded panel** in the comparison frame, label obscured.
- The Prime-native version of `S` is arithmetic, not a race: **the panel teardown.** *"Two panels. One number. Let's read them."* Split frame: Prime's supplement facts left, a generic 250mg panel right, both legible, finger tracing each figure as it's spoken.
- The other Prime-native version is **Bundle Math** (Pillar 4): $29.98 vs $16.66, 55¢ a day, arithmetic shown on screen, no percentages. 🔒 blocked on G4 until the capsule count in ads, on the PDP and in the box is one number.

## Framework 11 · **Triple G** — Goal · Gap · Gains

*Origin: Gratsi Wine / ButcherBox. Target: Unaware → Problem-Aware. Fastest framework to produce at volume.*

| Beat | Goal |
|---|---|
| **G** — Goal | Tap the specific outcome they want. Year-specific language for urgency |
| **G** — Gap | Expose why previous attempts failed; name the missing tool/knowledge |
| **G** — Gains | Present the product as the bridge; stack proof |

**Five hook shapes it generates:** Goal-Question (*"[Goal]? Let's change that."*) · Gap-Reveal (*"Still failing at [goal] because you're missing this"*) · Tool-Bridge (*"The best tool to [goal]"*) · Habit-Gap (*"Make [goal] a habit with [missing piece]"*) · Year-Goal (*"Your [year] [goal] starts with fixing this gap"*).

**Prime adaptation**
- Prime's `Goal` is never "anti-aging" and never a number. It is **a restored ability**: get through the afternoon without lying down · finish the job in an afternoon · put your pants on without holding onto something · be the one who does things, not the one who watches.
- Prime's `Gap` is the **depletion frame**, which is the single most useful positioning idea in the whole knowledge base: *"You can't raise it above normal. You can only get back to normal."* It explains who it works for, why some people feel nothing, why studies on healthy young people come back null, and why the plateau happens — all at once. Modest, which is the point.
- Better still, **replace the age axis with the depletion axis**: drinking, shift work, chronic stress, illness, hard training, menopause, poor sleep are all named in-thread as NAD+ drains. *"It's not your age. It's your last ten years."* This reaches people the category's own gatekeepers turn away, without contradicting them.
- ⚠ Year-specific urgency is fine. **Countdown urgency is not** — no starbursts, no "today only", no percentage.

## Framework 12 · **T.E.A.S.E.** — the Curiosity Loop

*Origin: The Essence Vault (Valentines). Target: Unaware. Highest retention structure in the set.*

| Beat | Goal |
|---|---|
| **T** — Tease | Open a loop with a surprising or impossible-sounding claim |
| **E** — Engage | Build story context; introduce the product without resolving the loop |
| **A** — Advantage | Stack benefits and social proof inside the narrative |
| **S** — Satisfy | Resolve the loop cleverly, making the opening claim logical |
| **E** — Encourage | CTA with a reason to act now |

**Why it works:** it creates a logical puzzle that can only be solved by watching to the end. Retention is structural, not performative.

**Prime adaptation — the natural home of Margaret**
- The strongest Prime loop is **involuntary discovery**: *"I didn't buy it for my skin."* The `T` beat states something that shouldn't follow; the `S` beat reveals it does. Placebo can't easily explain a benefit you weren't looking for — **and this audience knows it.** That is Proof Tier 3.
- Second-strongest loop: **the stop-and-notice test.** *"I stopped taking it for five days on purpose."* `S`: what she noticed. That is Proof Tier 4, and it is astonishingly confident — no other brand would dare suggest a pause.
- Third: **Tier 2, the animal.** The corpus's most-praised testimonial is a woman who dosed her limping dog before she'd try it herself. *"She wouldn't have placebo effect."* 🔒 needs a real, verified Prime customer with this story. Do not write one.
- ⚠ The `T` beat must not be an impossible *product* claim. Tease a **situation**, never a result.

## Framework 13 · Authority-Led "Mechanism Discovery & Identity Shift" · 6 beats

*Origin: Wine brand, $125K at 2.24 ROAS. Target: Problem/Solution-Aware — actively seeking, skeptical of traditional fixes, looking for a logical breakthrough.*
*Driver: authority validation → paradigm shift via a new mechanism → "so-that" experiential bridge → identity transformation.*

| # | Beat | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| ① | **Authority Hook + Painless Desire** | *"As a [authority], I tell my [audience] one thing: you can achieve [ultimate desire] without [hated friction]."* |
| ② | **Mechanism Genesis** | *"For years we were told [old dogma] was the only way. But when [breakthrough] surfaced, we discovered the real culprit isn't [old blame], it's [root problem]. To fix it you need [new mechanism]."* |
| ③ | **Schematic Bridge** (metaphor) | *"Think of [mechanism] like [everyday concept]. Just like [how it works], it [action], giving you [benefit] automatically."* |
| ④ | **Organic Market Discovery** | *"After seeing so many half-baked products claim this, [peer/research] pointed me toward [brand]."* |
| ⑤ | **"So-That" Experiential Bridge** | *"It uses [feature], so that you never deal with [pain], which means in real time you [daily action]."* |
| ⑥ | **Identity, Attachment & Close** | *"Because you're not someone who settles for [old compromise] anymore."* + experiential attachment + explicit CTA |

**Prime adaptation**
- ① **The authority must be true and stateable on screen.** A named formulator, the founder, or a credentialed reviewer whose credential you can display. No invented titles. Note that Prime's audience is **anti-biohacker** — she said so herself: *"I'd really like to hear from people who've actually used it, especially if you're not, like, a hardcore biohacker."* Cast accordingly.
- ③ **The best available schematic bridge for Prime is the recycling system:** *"Topping up a recycling system, not adding a new one."* It sidesteps the liver-conversion objection rather than arguing with it — you are not claiming delivery to a location, you are describing a system running slower than it used to. Second-best: the sleep bar fills, the energy arrow doesn't move.
- ④ 🔴 **HARD REFUSAL. Do not invent the referral.** In a corpus where *"Fyi, this user is an affiliate"* earned 31 upvotes, a fabricated "a colleague pointed me toward…" is precisely what this audience is scanning for. If no such person exists, cut the beat or replace it with a research disclosure the speaker makes about himself (*"So I read the panel before I read the ad."*).
- ⑤ The "so-that" chain is where Prime's claim ladder bites. `Supports cellular energy` → so that → **a behaviour that stopped**, never a metric that improved.
- ⑥ Identity close for Robert: *"I'm not trying to live to a hundred. I'm trying to finish the job in an afternoon."*

## Framework 14 · Solution-Aware "Alternative Disqualification" · 7 beats

*Origin: Intimacy brand, $200K at 2.3 ROAS. Target: Solution-Aware — they've tried the workarounds.*
*Emotional arc: Zone 3 (irritation/fatigue) → Zone 4 (deeper stakes/anxiety) → Zone 1 (calm/relief).*

| # | Beat | Zone | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | **Pattern Interrupt Hook** | Z3 | *"Stop pretending your [sensory adj] [workaround] actually works."* |
| ② | **Twist the Knife** | Z3 | *"They [annoyance 1], [annoyance 2], and you still end up with [unwanted outcome]."* |
| ③ | **Alternative Disqualification** | Z3 | *"[Alt 1] always [flaw]. [Alt 2] feels like [unflattering comparison]. Cheap [Alt 3] just lie to you."* |
| ④ | **Direct Solution Pivot** | — | *"The [product] actually fixes the problem."* |
| ⑤ | **Mechanism & Benefit Stacking** | — | *"The [feature 1] does [function]. The [feature 2] prevents [failure]. No [bad material], no [friction], no [common failure]."* |
| ⑥ | **Deep Emotional Agitation → True Desire** | Z4 | *"Now you don't have to [exhausting task] when you just want to [true desire]."* |
| ⑦ | **Reassurance Close & Offer Landing** | Z1 | *"[Core desire] should not give you [negative emotion]. You just need [product]. [Offer] + [X-day guarantee]."* |

**Prime adaptation — this is the account's single biggest untested opportunity**
- Beats ①–③ are the **category-quality contrast** in narrative form. That structure is 3 of Prime's 12 all-time winners and posts the **best CPM in the account — $57.47 vs $98.37** for the volume workhorse. At that spread, the same money buys **71% more impressions.** And it has **only ever been run on Astaxanthin (4% of revenue). It has never been tried on NMN (93.5%).**
- ③ disqualifies aggressively, which is why it works: a reader who doesn't already believe supplements vary in quality opts out at line one, so you stop paying for their click. **That is Law 1 in template form.**
- The Prime disqualification list writes itself from the corpus: *underdosed 250mg capsules · "fairy dusting" · no lot number · a manufacture date printed where an expiry date should be · proprietary blends · a COA that isn't tied to your batch.*
- ⚠ Beat ⑥ **literally instructs you to assert something about her** (*"you don't have to…"*). Rewrite as a question she answers internally or a disclosure the speaker makes about himself. Non-negotiable — see Law 4.
- ⑦ **Offer landing is per-bottle or per-day, never a percentage.** *"$29.98 for one. $99.98 for six — that's $16.66 each, 55¢ a day."* Guarantee above the fold, not in the footer.

## Framework 15 · The "Good / Better / Best" Triad — Status & Social Proof · 5 components

*Best suited to lifestyle, grooming, fashion and aesthetic products where the driver is status, romance or sensory validation.*

| # | Component | Structural formula | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Common Misconception** | The popular superficial advice the market repeats | *Beginner:* *"Just buy [popular brand]. Everyone uses it, you're fine."* |
| 2 | **The Half-Measure** | The common workaround, proving you understand intermediate frustration | *Average:* *"Nah, you have to [multi-step routine]. You can't just [basic method]."* |
| 3 | **The Sensory Scenario** | A high-status micro-story where the end benefit happened organically | *Pro:* *"Last week [person] leans in and says: '[exact compliment].' I didn't change the [core thing], I changed the vehicle."* |
| 4 | **Delivery Flaw vs Unique Vehicle** | Why traditional formats fail, and how yours solves it | *"Traditional [category] is [flawed vehicle], so it [loses effect] in [short time]. Ours uses [proprietary base] that [performs] for [duration]."* |
| 5 | **Value & Temporal Contrast** | Anchor price against the high-end alternative; highlight all-day performance | *"It's [brand]. Just [$price] a unit, and it gets you [outcome] at [late hour] when you used it at [early hour]."* |

**Prime adaptation — Margaret only, and handle component 3 carefully**
- Component 3 is the **third-party observation**, which is Proof Tier 5 and the strongest skin-proof shape available: *"people have been noticing the glow in my face!"* 🔒 needs a verified Prime customer. Never write one.
- ⚠ **Margaret's driver is identity, not vanity.** The report is explicit: copy that treats it as vanity will miss. She looks for herself first in a group photo. The mirror is lying, and the person she sees doesn't match the person she feels like. Register: **mildly puzzled, working it out as she says it.** If she sounds like she's delivering good news, it's wrong.
- 🔴 **Never lead with "tan" or "glow" as the angle.** That angle returns 0.98 ROAS at $59.96 CPA across 71 ads. Lead with the involuntary discovery; let the glow be what she found, not what she bought.
- 🔴 **No before/after. No beauty lighting. No ring light. No retouching.** Her own bathroom, the real overhead light.
- Component 5's temporal contrast for Prime is **the day, not the hour**: 55¢, six months, one decision.

## Framework 16 · The "Threat & Relief" Authority Bridge — Loophole & Performance · 6 components

*Best suited to performance supplements, technical skincare and offers where customers fear failing, wasting money, or crossing a compliance/health line.*

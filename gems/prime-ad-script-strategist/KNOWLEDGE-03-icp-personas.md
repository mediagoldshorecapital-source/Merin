## ICP 1 · LINDA — ENERGY · *the tired one who used to run everything*

**Who she is.** 62, female, sweet spot 58–68. American. Roughly 40% carry legacy email — AOL, Yahoo, Comcast,
Juno — meaning she adopted email 20–25 years ago and never moved. First names cluster 1940s–70s: Darlene, Polly,
Janis, Peggy, Maureen. Retired or working part-time. Married; **her husband regards her as fine.**

**Geography.** Sun Belt and retirement corridor. CA · FL · TX · NY · IL · OH · GA · AZ. **Arizona is the premium
market** — Tucson $82 AOV, Chicago $72, Henderson $67. Houston ($43) and Miami ($47) weakest.

### Her day, hour by hour — this is where hooks come from

| Time | What happens | Creative implication |
|---|---|---|
| **6:30 AM** | Awake after eight hours, **unrested**. The symptom that convinces her the problem is structural, not lifestyle | **The single best Problem-Aware entry point** |
| 7:00 | Coffee and the existing stack — she already takes six things and cannot attribute any of them | **Sell subtraction, never addition** |
| 10:30 | Sits down and scrolls. 11 AM is the 5th-ranked order hour | Shorter cuts, static and carousel |
| **2:00–4:00 PM** | **The crash lands. She considers a nap** | **The most ownable symptom in the category** |
| **6:00–7:00 PM** | Recliner, TV on, phone in hand, Facebook Reels. **The two highest order hours in the account** | The :20s UGC, unmuted, story-led |
| 10:30 PM | Bed. And she will wake tired again | — |
| 11 PM–1 AM | Unusually high ordering for this demographic | **Mechanism creative only. No offer, no urgency** |

### Deep insecurities, ranked

1. **I'm becoming invisible** — she sits while others do
2. I can't keep up with my grandchildren
3. My body stopped listening to me — post-menopause
4. I'm falling behind my friends
5. I've been scammed before
6. I can't afford to waste money
7. My husband doesn't understand

**Surface pain:** *"I'm tired all the time."*
**Real pain:** her body is betraying the person she still believes she is.

**Trigger moments.** Sitting down while everyone else keeps moving · the 3 PM crash · declining something she'd
once have said yes to · waking unrested after a full night · a grandchild's request answered with *"maybe later."*

**Job to be done.** *"Get me back to being the one who does things, not the one who watches."*

**Emotional register.** **Grief**, for the person she used to be, under a surface of tiredness. Not sadness —
**anger is the tell when it surfaces.**

### Her exact language — highest-scoring creative-ready lines

| Verbatim | Note |
|---|---|
| *"Used to crash hard at my desk around 2.30pm/3"* | **The most ownable symptom in the category.** Specific, shared, self-verifiable, **zero claim risk** |
| *"my energy would just crash by 4 pm everyday. I felt so much 'older' in the past 6 months. I would have to come home and just lie down for at least an hour."* | The lie-down detail is undignified, which is why it is believable |
| *"I should not feel like this"* | **Five words. The category's entire emotional core. Test it naked on a black card** |
| *"I woke up feeling like crap everyday. I'm fit, I'm healthy, I walk for miles every day, still woek part time and I should not feel like this."* | Anger, not sadness. **Note the typo. Keep it** |
| *"running a marathon breathing through a straw"* | Best metaphor in 161,000 words. **Campaign-level, not ad-level** |
| *"my energy is really bad in the mornings and only seems to get better at night which makes me stay up later"* | Names the vicious cycle. Underused |
| *"I'm sick of being tired, foggy and weak I can't exercise"* | *"Sick of"* is the emotional tell |
| *"I don't even take naps after work anymore"* | **Benefit stated as a behaviour that stopped. Strongest testimonial format there is** |

### Casting, tone and visual rules

- **Female 58–66. Never under 58** — G5 shows the audience rejecting younger casting out loud.
- **Register: flat, faintly irritated**, telling you a fact about her day she has stopped questioning. Not sad, not warm, not selling.
- Domestic locations, available light, **visible clutter**. Plain beats polished.
- **QC every asset at 4:5 and 9:16 on a 10-inch tablet at arm's length.**

**Best-friend card.** Linda, 62, Phoenix, retired teacher. Husband Bill, four grandchildren; the three-year-old is
everything. She wakes unrested, walks the dog at 8, and by evening she is in the recliner with the TV on and her
phone in her hand. She sees your ad, thinks *that is exactly how I feel*, and taps. She reads two reviews from
women who sound like her, picks a bundle and pays with Apple Pay in four minutes.

## ICP 2 · LINDA — CLARITY · *the one who is frightened of her own mind*

> ⚠ **THIS IS A CREATIVE HYPOTHESIS, NOT A MEASURED SEGMENT.** It cannot be sized or tied to revenue with any
> data available. It survives because the fear it describes is **the most acute in the research and the least
> addressed in the creative** — which is a reason to test it, not a reason to believe it. Learning budget only.

**Who she is.** Demographically indistinguishable from Linda–Energy. The difference is **motivation, not
profile.** Her fear is worst at night. She is the most likely of the four to research before buying — which makes
her the natural fit for the untapped search channel. **She reads the mechanism, not the offer.**

### Deep insecurities, ranked

1. **Am I losing my mind** — she watched her mother, aunt or friend, and every forgotten name lands as a cold wave
2. I'm running out of time
3. I'll become a burden to my children
4. Losing the word mid-sentence in front of people

**Trigger moments.** The witnessed blank · the pause at the dinner table while everyone waits · blanking on a
grandchild's name at a birthday · re-reading the same paragraph three times.

**Job to be done.** *"Give me a reason this is fixable and not the thing I watched my mother go through."*

**Emotional register.** **Fear**, sharp and specific, with **shame** attached to admitting it. She has not told
anyone, including her husband.

### Her exact language

| Verbatim | Note |
|---|---|
| *"feel the brain fog lift"* | **The best three words available.** Subtraction, not addition |
| *"I feel like I can formulate words quickly than before"* | Word-finding as a benefit. Badly underused. **Keep the grammar error** |
| *"all brain fog is gone and I have a mental clarity I otherwise only had during the first days after vacation"* | **The vacation benchmark is superb. Borrow the comparison** |
| *"foggy in the head more often than I'd like"* | Softer and more relatable than clinical "brain fog" |
| *"I've had tiredness and head fog for years"* | *"Head fog"* — the layman variant. Worth testing against "brain fog" |
| *"Now the body feels tired but brain is clear"* | Decouples physical from mental fatigue |

> ⚠ **The single hardest compliance constraint in the whole system lives on this persona.** See KNOWLEDGE-02 §4.
> Name the moment, never the diagnosis. **The witnessed blank, not dementia. No exceptions, in any voice.**

**Casting, tone and visual rules.** Female 58–66. Register: **searching, not panicking.** The most important
direction: **she is not distressed, she is looking for a word.** Dinner tables, kitchens, ordinary rooms. **Never
a clinical setting. Never a worried family member in frame.**

## ICP 3 · MARGARET — MIRROR · *the best customer you have, and you barely market to her*

**Who she is.** Same demographic core as Linda, **identified by behaviour rather than profile: she has bought
Astaxanthin alongside NMN.** The most valuable buyer in the business on every measure available. She buys energy
first and the mirror second.

**Why she is the best-evidenced persona of the four:** buying ASTA is an observable event. Everything about her is
**measured** rather than inferred — the opposite of every other persona in the set.

**How she finds you.** She does **not** usually arrive on an ASTA ad — she arrives on NMN and adds ASTA later.
**The attach is the mechanic, not the acquisition.** Her distinct moment is the mirror — catching her reflection
in a shop window, or seeing a photo she was tagged in. Not time-of-day bound, which makes her a **retargeting
opportunity, not a dayparting one.**

### Deep insecurities, ranked

1. **I look older than I feel inside** — the mirror is lying, and the person she sees does not match the person she feels like
2. I'm falling behind my friends
3. The same energy fears as Linda, underneath

> **This is an identity crisis, not vanity, and copy that treats it as vanity will miss.** The failure mode is
> already visible in the comment threads — G5, where a cosmetic-surgery-coded spokesperson produced **personally
> offended** replies from exactly these women.

**Trigger moments.** The reflection in a shop window · a photograph someone else tagged her in · a compliment she
did not expect · **looking for herself first in a group photo.**

**Job to be done.** *"Make the outside match the person I still feel like inside."*

**Emotional register.** **Grief moving to hope.** The mirror is the wound; the improvement is the reward.

### Her exact language

| Verbatim | Note |
|---|---|
| *"Nick Vujnovich thank you. I'm going to continue on an empty stomach. My skin looks better too."* | `OWN-92` — **the single most valuable sentence in Prime's own bank.** An involuntary discovery, from a real customer, about the exact benefit that defines the most valuable segment. **Skin improvement is being discovered as a side effect of NMN, not sold** |
| *"people have been noticing the glow in my face!"* | Third-party observation — the strongest skin proof shape. **Not publishable as yours** |
| *"Was is crepey or wrinkly before? Are you talking about the skin on your face or body or both?"* | Gives you the exact vocabulary — **"crepey"** — and the exact information gap |
| *"my finally-greying hair"* | *"Finally"* — greying framed as a long-delayed defeat |
| *"my lifelong dark under circles, always present regardless of how hydrated, how well rested, how good of a diet"* | The "I've tried everything" structure |

### Casting, tone and visual rules

- Female 58–68. **No makeup styling, no ring light, no beauty grade, no retouching.**
- **Register: mildly puzzled, working it out as she says it. If she sounds like she is delivering good news, it is wrong.**
- **Never a before/after. Ever, on this pillar.** Her own bathroom, the real overhead light.
- 🔴 **Never lead with "tan" or "glow"** — that angle returns 0.98 ROAS at $59.96 CPA across 71 ads.

**The commercial case, stated once.** Moving 10% of the 6,491 NMN-only customers into the ASTA attach adds roughly
$27.34 each ≈ **$17,700 of LTV, on traffic already paid for. Nobody has ever sent her an ad about it.**

## ICP 4 · ROBERT · *spends more, stays longer, and has never been advertised to*

**Who he is.** 60–72, retired professional, veteran or tradesman. Gmail and iCloud rather than AOL and Yahoo.
Phoenix, Las Vegas, Houston, San Diego. Buys $100+ bundles more frequently. More likely to research the spec
first.

> The research puts him at **1.81 ROAS, the most efficient demographic in the account.** That figure is `[R]` and
> is **not verifiable in the warehouse — confirm it in Ads Manager before budget moves.** Everything else about
> him is `[M]`.

### Deep insecurities, ranked

1. **I'm becoming my father** — he watched him slow down, lose his edge and become dependent, and refuses to let it happen
2. I've lost a step and I'm the only one who has noticed
3. I won't be a burden
4. Recovery takes days now

**His register is refusal, not repair. He will not describe any of this as fear.**

**Trigger moments.** A task that used to be easy taking noticeably longer · watching a peer decline · **the
Saturday job costing him Sunday** · reading a supplement facts panel and finding it short.

**Job to be done.** *"Keep me capable and independent, and don't make me ask for help."*

**Emotional register.** **Fear reframed as pride and defiance.**

### His exact language

| Verbatim | Note |
|---|---|
| *"Yes, 1,000 mg is the best dose. However, this is not Third party tested. Also not GMP."* | `OWN-61` — **the whole Robert objection in one comment, from a real customer, in public. And it is a solved problem the day the COA is on camera** |
| *"I ran out two months ago. I'm not that guy anymore."* | **The best male hook in the corpus.** Also a win-back subject line |
| *"Stopped for 2 months. I felt tired. I felt old."* | Flagship stop-and-restart testimonial. **Withdrawal as proof** |
| *"I can put my pants on without holding on to something."* | **The single best 65+ testimonial format: a restored ability, named exactly** |
| *"I'll be 57 and I can do more pushups than at 18."* | **Numbers beat adjectives, every time** |
| *"I felt old."* | Three words. Works as an opening frame or a closing card |
| *"There is no phone number to call. Don't trust companies that make you email"* | **Publishing a phone number is a trust asset, not a cost centre** |

> ⚠ All Golden-line quotes above are Reddit/YouTube provenance. **Research input for your own copy, not social
> proof you can publish.**

### Casting, tone and visual rules

- **Male 60–72, non-actor, audibly someone who has done physical work.**
- **Register: flat, unsentimental, slightly reluctant.** He is stating a fact he would rather not state. **No warmth in the read. If the take sounds moved, it is wrong. One take only — the first is always better on this register.**
- Workshop, garage, boat trailer, ladder. **Nothing shot in golden hour. Nothing aspirational.**
- **Spec converts him where story converts her.** Milligrams, purity, third-party testing, **in the first ten seconds. Give him something to read, uninterrupted — that silence is the qualification.**

**Best-friend card.** Robert, 66, Henderson. Retired tradesman. He watched his father stop driving at 71 and swore
he would not go that way. He is a step slower and nobody else has noticed yet. He will read a supplement facts
panel before he reads a headline, and he will buy the big bundle if the numbers hold up. **There is not one ad in
the account written to him.**

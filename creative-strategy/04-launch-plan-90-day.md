# 3. Naming convention

```
[MMDD]_[Persona]_[Awareness]_[Pillar]_[Angle]_[Format]_[Creator]_[Version]
```

**Examples:**
```
0912_LindaB_PA_Energy_3PMCrash_UGC_C04_v1
0912_Robert_UA_Identity_MyFather_UGC_M02_v1
0919_LindaB_PrA_Trust_ScamQ_Explainer_Founder_v1.1
0926_Dana_PA_Mind_230Meeting_UGC_C07_v2-15s
```

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Persona | `LindaB` `Dana` `Robert` `Returner` |
| Awareness | `UA` `PA` `SA` `PrA` `MA` |
| Pillar | `Energy` `Mind` `Trust` `Value` `Identity` `Mirror` |
| Format | `UGC` `Static` `Carousel` `Explainer` `Montage` `Founder` `Teardown` |
| Creator | your roster code — `C01`…, `M01`… for male creators |
| Version | `v1` · `v1.1` hook · `v1.2` intro · `v1.3` CTA · `v1.4` pacing/music · `v2` new edit · `v2-15s` length cut |

**This is not bureaucracy.** At month six you will have 300+ ads. Filtering ad names by
`_Trust_` and reading the aggregate CPA is how you learn that Trust outperforms Value by 30%
— which decides where next quarter's twelve concepts come from. Without the convention that
question is unanswerable.

# 5. Test design rules

| Rule | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Concepts live in testing at once | 6–8 | Fewer starves learning; more starves each ad |
| Judge engagement metrics at | **$28 spent** (1× target CPA) | Hook rate and CTR are readable this early |
| Kill with zero purchases at | **$85 spent** (3× target CPA) | Beyond this you're paying for a known answer |
| Declare a winner at | **50 purchases or $200 spent** | Below this, CPA is noise |
| Promote to scaling when | CPA ≤ $28 for 3 consecutive days | One good day is variance |
| Budget increase per step | ≤ 20–30% every 48–72h | Larger jumps reset learning |
| Variables changed per iteration | **exactly 1** | Otherwise the result teaches nothing |
| Expected hit rate | **~1 in 6** becomes a scaler | This is normal. Plan volume around it. |
| Retire creative at | frequency > 2.5 in the same audience | |

# 6. Timing — what your order data actually says

## Launch day: **Thursday**

| Day | Orders | Index |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | 742 | **Highest** |
| Thursday | 732 | Near-peak |
| Tuesday | 695 | Strong |
| Sunday | 679 | — |
| Wednesday | 674 | — |
| Friday | 673 | — |
| **Monday** | **575** | **Lowest — 22% below Saturday** |

Launching Thursday puts the learning phase across Thu–Fri–Sat, your three-day volume peak.
The ad set exits learning faster and on better data.

**Never launch a new ad set on Monday.** You will spend your lowest-volume day paying for a
slower, noisier learning phase.

## Dayparting: use it surgically, not everywhere

Your two buying peaks are **11 AM EST (299 orders/hr — your single best hour)** and
**7 PM EST (293/hr)**, with the 2–5 PM crash window at 229–252/hr.

**Do not daypart the scaling campaign.** Meta requires a lifetime budget for ad scheduling,
it fights Advantage+ delivery, and restricting hours on your main spend reduces the
algorithm's room to find cheap conversions. You will usually lose more to constrained
delivery than you gain from timing.

**Do run one small dayparted campaign** — `PI_MOMENT_3PM`, ~$150/day, lifetime budget,
scheduled 2:00–5:00 PM EST, carrying only C02 and S7.

**Why this one is worth the delivery penalty:** the 3pm crash creative is the only asset in
the account whose message is *literally about the moment it's being delivered in*. The report
puts it precisely — *"she's literally experiencing the problem as she sees the ad."* Nothing
else in the plan gets that lift from timing, so nothing else earns the constraint.

**Where the timing data pays off with no downside:**
- **Launch timing** — Thursday, and push new creative live in the morning so the 11 AM peak
  is inside day one.
- **Email/SMS sends** — 10:30 AM and 6:30 PM EST, no algorithmic cost at all.
- **Budget increases** — make them in the morning, not at night.

# 7. Placement and device

| Device | Sessions | CVR |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | 103,352 | 4.21% |
| **Tablet** | 3,387 | **5.11% — highest** |
| Desktop | 3,449 | 1.86% |

Tablet is only 3% of sessions but converts 21% better than mobile — she's in the recliner with
the iPad in the evening. It's small volume, but the fix is nearly free:

- **Export 4:5 and 1:1 versions of every winning concept**, not just 9:16. Tablet feed
  renders 4:5 far better than a letterboxed vertical.
- **Increase font size on all on-screen text.** Your buyer is 58–68. Text sized for a
  25-year-old's phone is a conversion tax on this audience — on every device.
- Don't exclude desktop; at 1.86% it's not worth the delivery restriction, and Advantage+
  placements will de-prioritize it on its own.

# 8. Production capacity — what this actually requires

| Resource | Wave 1 | Steady state |
|---|---|---|
| Net-new concepts | 8 | 8/month |
| Iterations | 0 | 10–14/month |
| Creators on roster | 5 | 8–10 (3+ male) |
| Statics/carousels | 3 | 4–6/month |
| Editing hours | ~25 | ~30/month |
| Strategist hours | ~20 (research) | ~8/month |

**The single biggest predictor of whether this plan works is whether you actually ship 8 new
concepts a month.** At a 1-in-6 hit rate, 8 concepts is 1.3 new winners per month — just
enough to outpace fatigue. At 3 concepts a month, you are shipping half a winner and the
account declines no matter how good the strategy document is.

If capacity is the constraint, cut in this order:
1. Cut iterations before net-new concepts. *(Wrong instinct, but right answer early — you
   need a portfolio before you have something worth iterating.)*
2. Shift the mix toward VO-montage and static concepts (C03, C13, all of § 3 in the hook
   bank) — no creator booking, produced in-house in a day.
3. Cut Wave 3 depth concepts before cutting Wave 1 gap-fillers.

**Never cut:** C04 (scam), C06 (Robert), C03 (Unaware VO). Those three are the P0 gaps, and
each one opens an audience the account currently cannot reach at any budget.

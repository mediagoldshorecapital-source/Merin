# Creator Brief — Prime Ingredients NMN

*Send this to the creator. It is deliberately non-technical.*

---

## What we're making

A short, honest video about your own experience — the kind of thing you'd send a friend, not
a commercial. **We are not looking for polish. We're looking for true.**

## Who's watching

A woman in her late 50s or 60s, sitting in her chair with her phone, usually with the sound
off. She has bought supplements before that did nothing and she's a little embarrassed about
it. She is looking for a reason not to believe you.

**The single best thing you can do is sound like someone she'd trust — not someone selling.**

---

## Setup

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Your own home. Living room, kitchen, or porch. Not a studio. Real light from a window. |
| **How to hold it** | Vertical. Phone propped up at chest height, not handheld. Arm's length. |
| **Sound** | Quiet room. No TV, no fan, no music. Your phone's mic is fine if the room is quiet. |
| **Wardrobe** | What you'd actually wear on a normal Tuesday. |
| **Light** | Face a window. Never have the window behind you. |

---

## How to deliver it

1. **Don't read.** We've sent you bullet points, not a script. Say it in your own words. If a
   sentence feels wrong in your mouth, change it.
2. **Start already talking.** No "Hi guys," no introduction, no waving. Start mid-thought.
3. **Do it 5 times.** Each take, start with a different opening line (we've listed 5 below).
   Then keep going into the rest.
4. **Pauses are good.** If something is hard to say, let it be hard. That's the take we'll use.
5. **One long take per attempt.** Don't stop and restart — we'll cut it.

## Things you must NOT say

- Don't say it **cures, treats, prevents, or reverses** anything
- Don't name any **disease or medical condition** — yours or a family member's
- Don't say **"you"** about the viewer's health. Say **"I."**
  - ✗ "If you're tired all the time…"  ✓ "I was tired all the time."
- Don't promise a **specific result in a specific time**
- Don't claim a **credential** you don't have

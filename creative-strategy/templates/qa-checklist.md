# Pre-Launch QA Checklist

Run before the ad goes live. **Also run § 2 before the shoot** — a compliance problem caught
at brief stage costs nothing; caught at launch it costs a reshoot.

---

## 1. Strategy

- [ ] Has a **Concept ID** and it names a real cell in the grid
- [ ] Fills a gap marked **P0/P1** on the Gap Scorecard
- [ ] The **awareness level of the copy matches** the awareness level in the name
      *(an "Unaware" ad that opens with the offer is mislabeled and will read as a failure of
      the wrong hypothesis)*
- [ ] Speaks to **one** persona, not a blend
- [ ] Changes **exactly one variable** vs. the version it iterates on

## 2. Compliance ⚠️

- [ ] No **second-person** statement about the viewer's health, age, or condition
      *(search the script for "you"/"your" next to any health word)*
- [ ] No **disease name** anywhere — spoken, on-screen, or in the primary text
- [ ] No **"cure / treat / prevent / reverse / clinically proven / doctor recommended"**
- [ ] No promise of a **specific result in a specific timeframe**
- [ ] No **before/after body** imagery
- [ ] No **fake UI** — fake play buttons, fake comments, fake notifications
- [ ] No **countdown or scarcity** that isn't real
- [ ] Every **number** on screen verified current: mg, %, review count, guarantee days, price
- [ ] Every **document/screenshot** shown is genuine and unaltered
- [ ] Every **press logo** corresponds to real, verifiable coverage
- [ ] **FDA disclaimer** present where required

## 3. Creative craft

- [ ] **First frame** is a face or the problem — not a logo, not black
- [ ] Hook is delivered **within 3 seconds**, and the noun arrives in second one
- [ ] On-screen text and spoken line **differ** (reinforcing, not identical)
- [ ] **Burned-in captions** throughout, not auto-captions
- [ ] Text is **high contrast** with a stroke or solid backing
- [ ] Text is **oversized** — legible on a 5-inch screen at arm's length, for a 58–68 viewer
- [ ] Text clear of the **top 250px / bottom 350px** safe zones
- [ ] There is a **turn** — a reframe — before the 15-second mark
- [ ] There is exactly **one** call to action
- [ ] Audio: VO clean, music ≥18dB under, no clipping
- [ ] Watched **on mute** start to finish and still makes complete sense

## 4. Technical

- [ ] 9:16 @ 1080×1920 exported
- [ ] 4:5 and 1:1 exported *(tablet converts at 5.11% — highest of any device)*
- [ ] 15s cut exported
- [ ] Filename = Concept ID
- [ ] Raw footage archived to the shared drive *(this is what makes hook iteration free)*
- [ ] Thumbnail/cover frame chosen deliberately

## 5. Launch setup

- [ ] Ad name follows the **naming convention** exactly
- [ ] In the correct campaign — **TESTING, not scaling**
- [ ] Destination URL correct, with UTMs
- [ ] Pixel/CAPI firing — tested with the Meta Pixel Helper
- [ ] Primary text: **first line stands alone** *(everything after is truncated on mobile)*
- [ ] Headline set
- [ ] CTA button matches the ad's ask
- [ ] Launching on a **Thursday**, in the morning — never Monday
- [ ] Comments enabled and someone is assigned to read them
      *(they're your best free VoC source and your fastest objection signal)*

---

## The 30-second final check

Watch it once on your phone, on mute, holding it the way she does.

**Three questions:**
1. In the first 3 seconds, did I know who this was for and what it's about?
2. At 15 seconds, do I want to know what happens next?
3. At the end, do I know exactly what to do and why I'd bother?

**Any "no" means it isn't ready.**

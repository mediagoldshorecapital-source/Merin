# NMN Hook & Script Bank

Everything here is written from the customer's own language in the Deep Customer Intelligence
Report. **Nothing was invented that a customer had already said better.**

**How to use this file:**
1. Pick a Concept ID from the grid in `02` § 6.
2. Take the matching script below.
3. Pull **5 hook alternates** from § 1 for that pillar and put all 5 in the brief.
4. Run the script through § 4 Compliance Rewrite Table.
5. Shoot. Test 3 hooks against the winning body.

> ⚠️ Every claim below marked **[VERIFY]** comes from the intelligence report's description of
> the product page and must be confirmed as current and substantiated before it airs.

**Never show a repeat buyer the single-bottle or BOGO offer.** VIP data: $60–85/order across
3–7 orders. They do not downgrade — don't invite them to.

# 4. COMPLIANCE REWRITE TABLE ⚠️

**Read this before every shoot.** Health is a restricted category on Meta. The two rules that
get beginners rejected — or account-restricted — are **personal attributes** and **disease
claims**.

## Rule 1 — Never assert or imply you know something about the viewer

Meta prohibits ad copy implying knowledge of the viewer's health, age, or condition. The fix
is almost always **move it to first person or third person.**

| ❌ Don't run | ✅ Run instead |
|---|---|
| "Are you struggling with brain fog?" | "I was struggling with brain fog." |
| "Feeling tired all the time?" | "I was tired in a way sleep didn't fix." |
| "Women over 60: your energy is declining" | "At 62, here's what changed for me." |
| "Your NAD+ levels are dropping" | "NAD+ falls by roughly half by 60." |
| "You can't keep up with your grandkids" | "I couldn't get on the floor with my grandson." |
| "Is your 3pm crash ruining your day?" | "The 3pm crash used to end my day." |
| "Tired of being tired?" | "I got tired of being tired." |

> **Note on the source material.** The creative strategy document proposes the hook *"When
> brain fog and afternoon fatigue hit right in the middle of your workday"* and *"Feeling
> more tired as you age?"* Both are second-person statements about the viewer's health and
> both carry rejection risk as ad copy. The *product page* can say them; the *ad* should not.
> Rewritten and used throughout this bank as: *"My 2:30 meetings were the problem"* and
> *"It's not just aging."* Same insight, no exposure.

## Rule 2 — Structure/function claims only. Never disease claims.

| ❌ Never say | ✅ Say |
|---|---|
| prevents / delays dementia, Alzheimer's | supports cognitive function |
| treats menopause / perimenopause | supports energy during midlife |
| cures chronic fatigue | supports healthy energy metabolism |
| reverses aging | supports healthy aging |
| lowers your risk of [disease] | *(cut entirely)* |
| doctor-recommended / clinically proven | *(only with a real, citable, held study)* |
| "my mother had dementia and I'm scared I will" | *(cut entirely — see below)* |

> 🚨 **The dementia line specifically.** The intelligence report's most emotionally loaded
> finding is *"I'm terrified I'm losing my mind like my mother did."* It is true, it is
> powerful, and it must **never appear in an ad.** Naming a relative's diagnosis alongside a
> supplement is a disease claim and an implied treatment claim in one sentence.
> **Use the observable moment instead:** *"The word just wouldn't come. Mid-sentence. In
> front of my daughter."* The fear is fully present. Nothing actionable was claimed.

## Rule 3 — Also prohibited or high-risk

- Before/after body imagery, weight-loss transformations
- Implied guaranteed results ("you WILL feel…"), unrealistic timelines
- Fake UI: fake play buttons, fake comment overlays, fake system notifications
- Countdown timers that reset, or scarcity that isn't real
- Press logos for coverage that doesn't exist — **verify Forbes/ELLE before use**
- Fabricated or composited reviews, COAs, or lab documents
- Missing the FDA disclaimer where required:
  *"These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This
  product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."*

## Pre-shoot compliance check (60 seconds, every script)

1. Search the script for **"you"** or **"your"** next to any health word. Rewrite to first person.
2. Search for any **disease name**. Delete it.
3. Search for **"cure", "treat", "prevent", "reverse", "clinically proven", "doctor recommended"**. Delete or substantiate.
4. Confirm every **number** (1000mg, 64%, 5,128 reviews, 60 days) is real and current.
5. Confirm every **document or screenshot** shown on camera is genuine.
6. Confirm the **disclaimer** is present where required.

**A rejection costs a day. A restricted ad account costs the business.** Never skip this.

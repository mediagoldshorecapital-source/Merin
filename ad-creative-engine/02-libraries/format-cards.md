# Format Cards — 10 Proven Ad Shapes

A **format** is the beat structure: how the ad is built once the hook has done its job.
The 31 competitor ads reduce to these ten. Each card gives the beats, the timing, the
production requirements, and where it breaks.

Pair a format with a hook archetype and an angle. Not every pairing works — the
"Pairs with" line on each card is the shortlist that has evidence behind it.

---

## F01 — Talking-Head Symptom Diagnosis
**Length:** 45–75 s · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware · **ICP:** Linda, Robert
**Pairs with:** H05, H06, H14 · **Angles:** A01, A02, A05

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:07 | Name the symptom with a timestamp; kill the false cause |
| Validation | 0:07–0:18 | "It's not your sleep / not your age" — remove blame |
| Mechanism | 0:18–0:38 | NAD+ decline, one analogy, one number |
| Product bridge | 0:38–0:52 | Dose, cofactors, testing — 3 specs maximum |
| Protocol | 0:52–1:02 | One capsule, morning, empty stomach |
| CTA | 1:02–1:12 | Price re-anchor + scarcity or bundle |

**Production:** one creator, one location, phone camera, natural light. Cheapest format in the library.
**Breaks when:** the mechanism section runs long. If you're past 25 seconds of biology with no product in sight, retention has already fallen off.

## F02 — Mechanism Explainer (whiteboard / motion graphic)
**Length:** 40–60 s · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware → Product-Aware · **ICP:** Robert
**Pairs with:** H06, H10, H03 · **Angles:** A02, A03, A04, A07

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:06 | A confusion or a failure to resolve |
| Diagram 1 | 0:06–0:20 | The pathway: NMN → NAD+, and what it consumes |
| The gap | 0:20–0:35 | Where the standard product fails (the bottleneck) |
| The fix | 0:35–0:48 | The complex closes the gap — show the label |
| CTA | 0:48–0:60 | Spec-forward, price-per-day |

**Production:** VO + motion graphics, or creator + pic-in-pic diagrams. Reusable asset library pays back over 10+ ads.
**Breaks when:** the diagram is accurate but not *legible at thumb size*. One idea per frame.

## F03 — Authority / Podcast Reaction
**Length:** 45–70 s · **Awareness:** Unaware → Problem-Aware · **ICP:** Both
**Pairs with:** H03, H04, H01 · **Angles:** A01, A06, A09

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Clip cold-open | 0:00–0:06 | The third-party soundbite plays first. No intro |
| Reaction | 0:06–0:14 | Creator reacts, restates the stake in plain words |
| Clip 2 / expansion | 0:14–0:30 | Second beat of the clip or the science |
| Translation | 0:30–0:45 | What it means for the viewer's Tuesday |
| Product | 0:45–1:00 | This is the version that matches what he described |
| CTA | 1:00–1:10 | |

**Production:** licensed or fair-use commentary clip + creator reaction. Legal check before shoot.
**Breaks when:** the clip is longer than 6 seconds before a human face appears. Also breaks if it reads as an endorsement — it must read as commentary.

## F04 — Peer Testimonial Montage
**Length:** 30–50 s · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware · **ICP:** Linda
**Pairs with:** H05, H07, H13 · **Angles:** A01, A05, A11

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| VO hook | 0:00–0:05 | "Here's what people noticed after 30 days" |
| Testimonial 1 | 0:05–0:14 | Energy / stamina — with lifestyle B-roll |
| Testimonial 2 | 0:14–0:23 | Skin / appearance |
| Testimonial 3 | 0:23–0:32 | Simplicity — replaced five bottles |
| Spec stack | 0:32–0:42 | Purity, dose, testing, made in USA |
| CTA | 0:42–0:50 | |

**Production:** 3–5 peer-aged creators, unbranded settings, warm light. The casting *is* the ad — the whole mechanism is "she looks like me."
**Breaks when:** the creators are visibly younger than the ICP, or the testimonials sound scripted. Let them stumble.

## F05 — Front-Porch UGC / Show-and-Tell
**Length:** 40–70 s · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware · **ICP:** Linda
**Pairs with:** H09, H05, H07 · **Angles:** A03, A05, A10

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Bottle in hand | 0:00–0:06 | Physical product on screen at frame 1 — legitimacy |
| Trust quick-stack | 0:06–0:16 | USA-made, lab-tested, open the bottle, show the capsule |
| Symptom agitation | 0:16–0:32 | Afternoon dips, sugar cravings, in her own words |
| Ingredient stack | 0:32–0:48 | Plain-language walkthrough |
| Peer recommendation | 0:48–1:00 | "I told my sister to get it" |
| Soft CTA | 1:00–1:10 | "Read about it yourself in the cart" |

**Production:** genuinely one take, phone, no edit polish. Polish kills this format.
**Breaks when:** it looks produced. If it has a lower-third, it isn't this format.

## F06 — Spec Comparison Teardown
**Length:** 50–75 s · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware → Product-Aware · **ICP:** Robert
**Pairs with:** H06, H10, H02 · **Angles:** A02, A03, A04, A08

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:06 | "Why didn't yours work? Go get your bottle." |
| Criterion 1 | 0:06–0:20 | Dose threshold — 1,000 mg vs. 250 mg |
| Criterion 2 | 0:20–0:34 | Cofactors present or absent |
| Criterion 3 | 0:34–0:46 | Third-party tested, no proprietary blends |
| Score card | 0:46–0:58 | Visual side-by-side, competitor unnamed/blurred |
| CTA | 0:58–1:12 | Price per gram, bundle math |

**Production:** two bottles on a counter, hands-only works fine. Or an on-screen score table.
**Breaks when:** you name or clearly identify a competitor brand. Blur labels; compare to "typical shelf products."

## F07 — Before / After Personal Transformation
**Length:** 50–80 s · **Awareness:** Unaware → Problem-Aware · **ICP:** Linda
**Pairs with:** H07, H01, H13 · **Angles:** A05, A11, A12

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Photo hook | 0:00–0:06 | The two photos, ages stated as numbers |
| Vulnerability | 0:06–0:20 | What she was worried about; what she'd tried |
| Discovery | 0:20–0:34 | How she found it — regulatory or authority story |
| Felt-state stack | 0:34–0:52 | Skin, energy, sleep, vitality — three sensations minimum |
| Demographic callout | 0:52–1:04 | "If you're a woman over 50 and you're not doing this…" |
| Commitment close | 1:04–1:15 | "Give it 30 days" |

**Production:** real customer, real photos, signed release, documented timeline.
**Breaks when:** anything is retouched or the lighting differs between photos. Category viewers are highly calibrated to fake before/afters — one tell and the whole ad dies.

## F08 — Founder / Insider Direct
**Length:** 40–60 s · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware → Product-Aware · **ICP:** Both
**Pairs with:** H10, H06, H14 · **Angles:** A03, A04, A08, A10

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Question hook | 0:00–0:07 | "People ask me this constantly:" + the real question |
| Authority frame | 0:07–0:16 | Why I'm the person answering it |
| The honest answer | 0:16–0:38 | Including what the product *doesn't* do |
| Why we built it | 0:38–0:52 | Formulation decision as a story |
| CTA | 0:52–1:02 | Low pressure, "go read the label yourself" |

**Production:** office or warehouse, one camera. Candour is the mechanism — admit a limitation out loud.
**Breaks when:** it becomes a brand video. This is a person answering a question, not a company talking.

## F09 — Anthropomorphic Skit / Category Duel
**Length:** 45–75 s · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware (Solution-Unaware) · **ICP:** Both
**Pairs with:** H11 · **Angles:** A06, A07, A13

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Confrontation | 0:00–0:08 | False solution challenges the product |
| Illusion vs. reality | 0:08–0:24 | "You get them 90 minutes." Crash, jitters, dependency |
| Loop redefinition | 0:24–0:42 | "That's not a boost, that's a cycle." |
| False comfort rebuttal | 0:42–0:56 | "That's a routine." / "It's a survival strategy." |
| Pivot + CTA | 0:56–1:08 | Direct habit swap |
| Banter tag | 1:08–1:15 | Comedy button — drives shares and rewatch |

**Production:** two props, static shot, two VO artists, simple mouth animation or puppetry. Higher effort, high reuse (swap the false solution: energy drink → coffee → nap → "pushing through").
**Breaks when:** the writing isn't actually funny. A flat skit is worse than no skit.

## F10 — Comment Reply / Native Q&A
**Length:** 30–50 s · **Awareness:** any · **ICP:** both
**Pairs with:** H09, H10 · **Angles:** any

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Read the comment | 0:00–0:06 | Screenshot on screen, read verbatim |
| Concede something true | 0:06–0:16 | Validate the premise; lowers guard |
| The reframe | 0:16–0:32 | The thing they had wrong |
| Product as the answer | 0:32–0:44 | Directly answers the asked question |
| CTA | 0:44–0:52 | |

**Production:** cheapest format that exists. Screenshot + phone camera. Should be the format you can ship in 24 hours when a comment thread pops off.
**Breaks when:** the comment is obviously fabricated. Use real ones — they're funnier and more specific than anything you'd write.

# Angle Map — 16 Angles

An **angle** is the *argument* the ad makes. Hook = how you stop them. Format = how you
build it. Angle = **why they should believe you.** This is where "what ads should we
make and why" is actually answered.

Each angle carries: the claim, the mechanism that supports it, the objection it defeats,
who it's for, and its current status.

**Status values:** `PROVEN` (a winner exists) · `TESTING` (live, no verdict) ·
`UNTESTED` (in the bank, never run) · `FATIGUED` (declining, rest 8 weeks)

---

## Tier 1 — Mechanism-of-Failure angles
*The highest-leverage cluster in this category. They target people who already bought a
competitor product and got nothing — the most motivated buyers in the market, and the
ones with the lowest acquisition cost, because they're already educated.*

### A01 — The NAD+ Decline Curve
**Claim:** the reason your energy changed is a measurable, age-related drop in a specific coenzyme.
**Mechanism:** NAD+ falls substantially from your 30s onward; it's the currency every cell uses to make energy and run repair.
**Defeats:** "It's just my age" (Objection 1 in disguise).
**ICP:** Linda (primary), Robert · **Awareness:** Unaware → Problem-Aware
**Best pairings:** F01/H05, F04/H05, F08/H08
**Status:** `PROVEN` — the backbone angle; nearly every competitor winner contains it.

### A02 — Underdosing / The 1,000 mg Threshold
**Claim:** most bottles are fairy-dusted; below a real serving the biology doesn't move.
**Mechanism:** the dose used in research is a full gram, not 250 mg.
**Defeats:** "I tried NMN and felt nothing."
**ICP:** Robert (primary), Linda · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware
**Best pairings:** F06/H06, F02/H06
**Status:** `PROVEN` — highest-conviction angle for Robert.

### A03 — The Methyl Donor Gap (TMG)
**Claim:** NMN consumes methyl groups to convert. Take it alone and you create a second deficit.
**Mechanism:** TMG (trimethylglycine/betaine) supplies the methyl group the conversion spends.
**Defeats:** "I'll just buy the cheap single-ingredient one."
**ICP:** Robert · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware → Product-Aware
**Best pairings:** F02/H02, F06/H06
**Status:** `PROVEN` — the single strongest competitor-disqualifying argument found in the teardown. It makes every single-ingredient SKU on the shelf look incomplete.

### A04 — The Leaky Bucket (cofactors & absorption)
**Claim:** a single ingredient without its cofactors is a bucket with a hole in it.
**Mechanism:** the complex includes the enzyme support and cofactors that keep the conversion from bottlenecking.
**Defeats:** "Why is yours more expensive than raw powder?"
**ICP:** Robert · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware
**Best pairings:** F02/H06, F06/H10
**Status:** `PROVEN`

### A05 — Visible Vanity ROI
**Claim:** cellular repair shows up in the mirror — skin tightness, texture, the jawline.
**Mechanism:** energy for repair and clearance of cellular debris.
**Defeats:** "Cellular health is too abstract for me to care about."
**ICP:** Linda · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware
**Best pairings:** F07/H07, F04/H07
**Status:** `PROVEN` — the necessary translation layer. Linda does not buy mitochondria; she buys the photo of herself she'd be willing to post.

## Tier 2 — Positioning & category angles

### A06 — The Stimulant Dependency Cycle
**Claim:** caffeine doesn't make energy, it hides fatigue. The crash is the loan coming due.
**Defeats:** "I'll just have another coffee."
**ICP:** both · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware, Solution-Unaware
**Best pairings:** F09/H11, F10/H09, F01/H05
**Status:** `PROVEN`

### A07 — NMN vs. NAD+ (precursor vs. end molecule)
**Claim:** they're not interchangeable; one is the raw material your body can actually use.
**Defeats:** "Should I just buy NAD+ directly?"
**ICP:** Robert · **Awareness:** Product-Aware
**Best pairings:** F02/H10, F08/H10
**Status:** `PROVEN` — best retargeting angle in the bank.

### A08 — Retire the Shelf (poly-pill elimination)
**Claim:** one complex replaces five bottles, and you'll actually take it.
**Defeats:** "Another thing to remember, another expense."
**ICP:** Linda (primary), Robert · **Awareness:** any
**Best pairings:** F05/H05, F04, F08
**Status:** `PROVEN` — badly underused. Compliance is the real reason supplements fail and nobody in the category talks about it honestly.

### A09 — Regulatory History / Forbidden
**Claim:** it was restricted, then reinstated — and the reason it was contested is that it works.
**Defeats:** "If it were this good I'd have heard of it."
**ICP:** both · **Awareness:** Unaware
**Best pairings:** F03/H01, F07/H01
**Status:** `PROVEN` — highest stop-rate, highest legal risk. See `proof-and-claims.md` before writing a word of it.

### A10 — Purity, Testing & Provenance
**Claim:** made in the USA, third-party lab tested, COA available, no proprietary blends.
**Defeats:** "Is this safe? Who actually makes this?"
**ICP:** both · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware → Product-Aware
**Best pairings:** F05/H05, F06/H06, F08/H14
**Status:** `PROVEN` — rarely the lead angle, mandatory as a supporting beat in every ad.

### A11 — Peer Demographic Proof
**Claim:** women exactly your age, in their own kitchens, at 30 and 90 days.
**Defeats:** "That worked for her, she's 30 / she's paid."
**ICP:** Linda · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware
**Best pairings:** F04/H05, F07/H07
**Status:** `PROVEN`

## Tier 3 — Underexploited / expansion angles
*Where next quarter's winners come from. Every one of these was visible in the competitor
set but only lightly worked.*

### A12 — Price De-anchoring vs. Clinical NAD+
**Claim:** an IV drip at a longevity clinic runs into the hundreds. Two months of capsules is a fraction of one session.
**Defeats:** "It's expensive."
**ICP:** both · **Awareness:** Solution-Aware
**Best pairings:** F01, F06, F08 · always as the closing beat
**Status:** `PROVEN` as a beat, `UNTESTED` as a full lead angle. **Recommended test.** Never defend price — move the reference point.

### A13 — The Cost of the Lost Afternoon
**Claim:** the thing you lost isn't energy, it's the four hours after 2 PM — every day, for years.
**Defeats:** "It's not that big a deal."
**ICP:** Linda · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware
**Best pairings:** F01/H05, F09/H11, F07/H08
**Status:** `UNTESTED`. **High-priority wild.** Reframes a symptom as a compounding loss, which is a far bigger emotional number than "tired."

### A14 — Recovery & Strength Retention
**Claim:** it's not that you got weaker, it's that recovery slowed — so you train less, so you get weaker.
**Defeats:** "I already work out, I'm fine."
**ICP:** Robert · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware
**Best pairings:** F01/H14, F06/H13
**Status:** `UNTESTED`. Opens the active-male segment, which the current slate barely touches.

### A15 — Bone Density & Independence
**Claim:** below the fatigue conversation is a structural one — the builders slow down before the tear-down crew does.
**Defeats:** "Energy isn't my priority."
**ICP:** Linda (60+) · **Awareness:** Problem-Aware
**Best pairings:** F02/H12, F07/H13
**Status:** `UNTESTED`, **high risk.** Strongest emotional pull in the whole map (autonomy, not vanity) and the tightest compliance constraints. Legal review before it's briefed, not after.

### A16 — The Morning Protocol Ritual
**Claim:** the reason your last supplement failed is that you stopped taking it. One capsule, one time, one trigger.
**Defeats:** "I'm bad at being consistent."
**ICP:** both · **Awareness:** any
**Best pairings:** F05/H09, F08/H10
**Status:** `UNTESTED`. Low-ceiling, high-floor. Excellent retargeting and email-companion angle.

## Voice of Customer — verbatim bank

Refill this every Tuesday from reviews, comments and support tickets. Verbatim customer
language outperforms written copy consistently; these lines go into scripts unedited.

**Harvesting rules**
- Copy the phrasing exactly. "I'm so tired of being tired" is better than anything you'd write.
- Log the source (review / comment / DM) and the date.
- A phrase said by three different customers is an angle, not a quote — promote it above.
- Objections are worth more than praise. Praise confirms; objections generate.

# Prompt — Write the Brief & Script

**Attach:** `03-templates/creative-brief.md` · `03-templates/script-template.md` ·
`02-libraries/format-cards.md` · `02-libraries/hook-bank.md` · `02-libraries/angle-map.md` ·
`02-libraries/proof-and-claims.md` · `00-system/icp-profiles.md` · the reference swipe ad

**Inputs to specify:** Format `Fxx` · Hook archetype `Hxx` · Angle `Axx` · ICP · Awareness ·
Runtime · Slate bucket · Parent creative (if an iteration)

---

You are writing a production-ready brief for **Prime Ingredients**. A creator with no
context must be able to shoot this without asking a single question.

Produce the **complete filled brief** and the **complete filled script**, in the exact
structure of the attached templates.

Non-negotiables:

1. **The first 3 seconds get disproportionate attention.** Write the spoken line
   verbatim, describe frame 1 literally (what is on screen at 00:00 — no walk-ins, no
   logos, no empty rooms), write the OST in ≤ 7 words so it carries the hook alone when
   muted, and specify what moves in the first half-second. Then run the four-point hook
   self-check and state pass/fail on each.
2. **Beat structure comes from the format card.** Match its timings. Product may not
   appear before the mark the awareness level allows.
3. **Specificity everywhere.** Exact times ("2:30"), exact numbers ("1,000 mg"), exact
   ages. Vague copy is the single most common cause of a flat hook rate.
4. **Language comes from the ICP's internal monologue**, not from a copywriter's voice.
   Pull phrasing from `icp-profiles.md` and the VoC bank.
5. **Handle at least two objections** from the ICP's objection stack, and say in the
   brief which beat handles which.
6. **Every claim goes in the claims table with its lane and its fix.** Flag anything
   needing legal review. If the angle requires a claim you cannot substantiate, say so
   and propose the nearest green-lane substitute rather than writing it anyway.
7. **Write the three Phase-6 hook variants** so the whole set can be batch-shot in one
   session.
8. **Write ad copy:** 3 primary-text variants (first line must survive truncation) and
   2 headlines ≤ 40 characters.

End with: the one-sentence hypothesis, and the single biggest reason you think this ad
might fail.

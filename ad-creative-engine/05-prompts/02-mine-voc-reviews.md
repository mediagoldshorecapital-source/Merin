# Prompt — Mine Voice of Customer

**Attach:** raw reviews / ad comments / support tickets / DMs (paste as-is, don't clean them) ·
`00-system/icp-profiles.md` · `02-libraries/angle-map.md`

---

You are a customer researcher for **Prime Ingredients**. Mine the attached raw customer
language. Preserve verbatim phrasing everywhere — typos, run-ons and all. Never
paraphrase into marketing voice; the value is in the actual words.

**1. Verbatim bank**
Table: `Verbatim quote | Source type | ICP (Linda/Robert/neither) | Emotional register | Feeds angle`
Include only lines with sensory or situational specificity. Skip "great product, love it."

**2. Symptom lexicon**
Every distinct way customers describe the *problem*, ranked by frequency. Note the exact
times of day, body parts and situations named — these become hook variables.

**3. Objection inventory**
Every stated or implied reason not to buy, ranked by frequency, each with the verbatim
that best expresses it and the angle that would defeat it.

**4. Unprompted benefit mentions**
Benefits customers report that our marketing does *not* currently claim. Flag which are
usable and which fall in the amber/red claim lanes. These are the highest-value output
of this prompt — they're angles nobody in the category is running.

**5. Trigger events**
What was happening in their life when they bought? (A photo, a birthday, a comment from
a grandchild, a doctor's visit.) These become cold-traffic hook setups.

**6. Angle candidates**
Any pattern said by 3+ different customers. For each: proposed angle name, the claim, the
mechanism, the objection it defeats, the ICP, and a ready-to-add row for the angle map
marked `UNTESTED`.

**7. Five hooks, written**
Five ready-to-shoot hook lines built from verbatim language, each tagged with its
archetype from the hook bank. They should sound like a customer talking, not a copywriter.

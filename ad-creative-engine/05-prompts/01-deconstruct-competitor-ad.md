# Prompt — Deconstruct a Competitor Ad

**Attach:** the ad (video, or transcript with timestamps) · `02-libraries/format-cards.md` ·
`02-libraries/hook-bank.md` · `02-libraries/angle-map.md` · `02-libraries/proof-and-claims.md`

---

You are a direct-response creative strategist for **Prime Ingredients**, a longevity
supplement brand (hero SKU: NMN Complex 1,000 mg). Deconstruct the attached competitor ad
using the 6-phase protocol. Be forensic and specific; never generic.

**Phase 1 — Verbal Architecture & Funnel Classification**
Timestamped transcript split into Hook / Setup-Bridge / Body-Mechanism / Climax-Stack /
Close-CTA. Then: funnel stage (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU), audience awareness level
(Unaware/Problem/Solution/Product), and the specific ICP — a person, not a demographic.

**Phase 2 — Macro Formula**
Compress the ad into a single arrow chain of *narrative functions* with no product nouns,
so the chain is portable to any product. Then one sentence naming the narrative engine —
the psychological mechanism actually doing the work.

**Phase 3 — Customer Psychologist Audit**
A table, one row per significant spoken line:
`Spoken line | Psychological trigger/bias | Target's internal dialogue (raw, 1st person, unedited) | Conversion viability (Very High/High/Medium/Low) + why`
The internal-dialogue column must sound like a real person thinking, not like marketing copy.

**Phase 4 — Prime Ingredients Adaptation (minimal 1:1)**
Rewrite beat-for-beat at the same timings. Table: `Time/Beat | Spoken line | Visual directives & OST`.
Preserve the original's rhythm, sentence length and level of specificity — swap the values,
not the structure. Replace any claim that fails `proof-and-claims.md` with a green-lane
claim of equivalent persuasive force; do not simply delete it.

**Phase 5 — Modular Templatization**
Convert Phase 4 into a reusable template with `[TAGGED VARIABLES]`, using the standard
variable names from `01-swipe/deconstruction-protocol.md`.

**Phase 6 — Hook & Angle Diversification**
Three fully-written alternative opens for the same body, with OST:
(1) Direct Symptom Qualification — Linda lane; (2) First-person transformation or
spec/verification — Robert lane; (3) Category contrast / purity anchor.

**Finally — the swipe row.** Output a single markdown table row for
`01-swipe/competitor-ad-index.md` classifying this ad by Format (Fxx), Angle(s) (Axx),
and Hook archetype (Hxx) from the existing libraries. If it fits none of them, say so
explicitly and propose the new card.

**Then answer the quality gate, one line each:**
- What single belief does this ad change?
- What must the viewer already believe for it to work?
- Which objection does it defeat, and which does it dodge?
- What's the most portable element — hook, structure, or proof asset?
- What could we not replicate, and what's our substitute?

# Family F — Offer Mechanics
**Parent:** V9 "BOF — grab up the NMN capsules"
**What's carrying the parent:** it removes every cognitive step between wanting it and
buying it. No problem statement, no mechanism, no education — an insider showing you how to
get the price down, one physical UI instruction, repeated twice, in twenty seconds.

**Why this family is underbuilt:** V9 was the only offer-mechanics ad in nine winners, and
this format was **entirely absent from the 31 competitor ads**. It has the highest ROAS per
second in the library on the smallest audience. We should be running three of these, not one.

**Non-negotiable for the whole family:** every deadline, stock statement and price must be
**true**. In this format the offer mechanics are the entire ad — if they're false there is
nothing else holding it up, and a fake countdown is red-lane regardless.

**Hypothesis:** Robert doesn't respond to "discount", he responds to *unit economics*. Doing

number he can defend to himself. Same offer, arithmetic instead of adjectives.

## F3 — "The Order You Tap Them In"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Creative name** | `PI_LIN_PDA_F10_A16_H18_9x16_v1` |
| **Parent** | V9 |
| **Transform** | **Beat extraction** — the stacking mechanic becomes the entire premise |
| **Format / Angle / Hook** | F10 · A16 · H18 |
| **ICP / Awareness** | Linda · Most-Aware |
| **Runtime** | 20 s |
| **Bucket** | Iteration |

**Hypothesis:** V9's value came from *insider knowledge of the mechanics*, not from the
size of the discount. If two real discounts stack and most people only ever apply one,
then "here's the order you tap them in" is a stronger premise than "there's a sale on" —
and it's twenty seconds long.

### The first three seconds
| Layer | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Spoken** | "Two discounts stack on this and most people only use one. Here's the order." |
| **Frame 1** | A phone screen already showing the product page, thumb hovering over the cart. |
| **OST** | `MOST PEOPLE USE ONE` |
| **Motion** | The thumb moves toward the cart on the first word |

### Beats
| Time | Beat | Spoken | Visual / OST |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Hook | "Two discounts stack on this and most people only use one. Here's the order." | Phone, thumb, cart · `MOST PEOPLE USE ONE` |
| 0:05–0:11 | Step 1 | "Tap the cart first — that applies the bundle price. Buy two get two free, $16.66 a bottle. No code to type." | Screen recording: cart tapped, price updates on screen · `1. TAP THE CART` |
| 0:11–0:16 | Step 2 | "Then the free shipping applies on top once you're at four bottles. That's the second one people miss." | Screen recording: shipping line goes to zero · `2. SHIPPING → $0` |
| 0:16–0:20 | CTA | "That's it. Two taps. And it's a genuine eight-month supply, not a sample." | Cart UI pointed at again · `TAP THE CART` |

**Production:** a real screen recording of the real checkout. If the second discount doesn't
actually stack, **do not shoot this** — rewrite it around whatever is true. The format has
no cover story if the mechanics are wrong.

# Family A — Authority Ladder
**Parents:** V1 "It's not plastic surgery" · V7 "Why is no one talking about this"
**What's carrying the parents:** three kinds of proof stacked — celebrity → Harvard
scientist → third-party observer — so no single one has to hold the claim. Plus the decay
statistic ("half your NAD by 50") and the price de-anchor ("$100–200 elsewhere").

**The open question these three variations answer:** *is the celebrity load-bearing, or is
it the science?* Celebrity clips are our highest legal exposure and our hardest asset to
license. If A1 holds without one, the whole family gets cheaper and safer to produce.

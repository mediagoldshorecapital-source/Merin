# Family B — The Comparison Dilemma
**Parents:** V2 "NAD or NMN??" · V6 "Which one of these is the best" · V8 "Anti-aging: NAD or NMN"
**What's carrying the parents:** the molecular-size / bioavailability argument. It runs in
three of our nine winners and it is the only argument we make that disqualifies an entire
competing shelf without naming a single brand. Plus a credential in the first eight
seconds and the 10-in-1 stack read aloud.

**The open question:** these three parents all *state* the argument. None of them make the
viewer **participate** in it. B1 tests participation.

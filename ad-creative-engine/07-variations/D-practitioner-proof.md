# Family D — Practitioner Proof
**Parent:** V3 "Before/After Nurse"
**What's carrying the parent:** the staccato before/after open (six cuts in eleven
seconds), a real credential arriving at 0:25 *after* the proof rather than before it, the
decay statistic, and the flawed-alternatives ladder (liposomal → injections → precursor)
which quietly does the price de-anchoring against IV therapy.

**The problem with the parent:** *"I'm literally aging in reverse"* is a red-lane product
claim. D1 keeps the visual proof and replaces the claim with something stronger — a
verifiable production condition the viewer can check for themselves.

**Hypothesis:** category viewers are highly calibrated to fake before/afters, so the
credibility bottleneck is not the transformation — it's whether they believe the photos.
Naming the production conditions out loud ("same lamp, same phone, no filter") should beat
a bigger claim with unexplained photos, because it hands them a way to audit us.

One tell and the ad dies and takes the account's credibility with it.

## D3 — "What I Stopped Doing in My Fifties"

| | |
|---|---|

| **Parent** | V3 |

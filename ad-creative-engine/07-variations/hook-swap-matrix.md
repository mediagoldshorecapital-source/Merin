# Hook Swap Matrix — V1–V9 × 3 Concepts

**27 hook swaps.** Each winner keeps its body exactly as it ran and gets three new first-six-seconds.
Nothing after the hook changes, so any movement in performance is attributable to the open alone —
that is the entire point of a swap matrix and the reason it is the cheapest test we run.

---

## Market grounding

Every swap borrows an archetype that is already proven — either in the **31 competitor ads**
(`competitor-ad-index.md`) or in **our own nine winners** (`winners-index.md`). Each concept cell
names the archetype and its evidence count, so no swap is a guess.

| Archetype | In the 31 market ads | Read |
|---|---|---|
| H07 Visual Age Contrast | 5 | Category default proof device. Gated on real assets |
| H05 Symptom Qualification | 5 | The volume workhorse — cheapest, most reliable |
| H01 Forbidden / Regulatory | 4 | Highest stop-rate, highest legal load |
| H03 Authority Name-Drop | 4 | Strong on cold traffic, needs clip rights |
| H09 Comment Reply · H10 Category Confusion · H14 Practitioner · H04 Celebrity · H08 Observation · H02 Elite | 2 each | Underused relative to performance |
| H06 Diagnostic Failure · H11 Duel · H12 Silent Threat · H13 Quantified Proof | 1 each | Each a viable wild |
| **H15 Ironic Warning · H16 Usage Intercept · H17 Physical Lineup · H18 Deal Arbitrage** | **0** | **Absent from the market set — these came from our own winners** |

## The swap logic

Each winner's three concepts are deliberately spread, never three rewordings of one idea:

- **Concept A — sharpen the same lane.** Closest to the parent, lowest variance. This is the control.
- **Concept B — transplant from the market.** An archetype proven in the competitor set but never run on this body. The real test.
- **Concept C — shift the entry point.** Different ICP or awareness level, widening who the same body can reach.

**Compliance:** ⚠ marks a swap carrying elevated claim or licensing risk. Rules in `proof-and-claims.md`.

## How to run the matrix

1. **Shoot in batches by parent.** All three hooks for one winner cost one extra session block — typically four to six minutes of the creator's time. The body already exists; you are producing 4–6 second files.
2. **Keep hooks as separate assets** in `/hooks/`. A new ad is then a file swap, not a shoot.
3. **One variable only.** Nothing after 0:06 changes. If the body is also edited, it is not a hook swap and the result is not attributable.
4. **Read hook rate first, at low spend.** Hook rate and hold-to-25% stabilise fast because they're measured on impressions — act on those before CPA, per `04-testing/testing-framework.md`.
5. **Promote and re-run.** A winning hook gets re-tested on a *different* parent body. That is how a swap matrix compounds: 9 bodies × the hooks that win becomes a much larger space than 27.

**Expected outcome, stated honestly:** in a matrix this size, roughly a third typically beat their parent's open, a third land flat, and a third lose. The value is not in any one concept — it's that after one round you know which archetypes your audience responds to, and every brief after that is written with evidence instead of taste.

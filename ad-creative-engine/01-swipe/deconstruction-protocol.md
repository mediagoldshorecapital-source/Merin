# The 6-Phase Deconstruction Protocol

How a competitor ad becomes a Prime Ingredients asset. Run this on every ad you add to
the swipe file. It takes about 40 minutes by hand, or a few minutes with
`05-prompts/01-deconstruct-competitor-ad.md`.

The discipline that makes it work: **you are not copying the ad, you are extracting the
machine underneath it.** The output of Phase 5 is a machine. Phases 1–4 are how you find it.

---

## Phase 1 — Verbal Architecture & Funnel Classification

Transcribe with timestamps and split into the six beats:
`Hook · Setup/Bridge · Body/Mechanism · Climax/Stack · Close/CTA`

Then classify:
- **Funnel stage** — TOFU / MOFU / BOFU
- **Awareness level** — Unaware / Problem / Solution / Product
- **ICP** — who is this actually for, in specifics, not demographics

*Why:* awareness level determines where the product may enter. Most failed adaptations
fail here — they lift a Product-Aware script and run it on cold traffic.

---

## Phase 2 — Macro Formula & Narrative Extraction

Compress the entire ad into a single arrow chain of *functions*, with no product nouns:

> Billionaire Curiosity Hook → Biological Bottleneck Reveal → Biochemical Logic Bridge →
> Multi-Action Formula Stack → Fraction-of-Cost Price Anchor & Scarcity Close

Then one sentence on the **narrative engine** — the psychological trick doing the work.

*Why:* the arrow chain is portable. It survives a change of product, ICP, and category.
This is the reusable asset; the script is disposable.

---

## Phase 3 — Customer Psychologist Audit

For each significant spoken line, four columns:

| Spoken line | Trigger / bias activated | Target's internal dialogue (raw, 1st person) | Conversion verdict |
|---|---|---|---|

The internal-dialogue column is the point. Write it as the customer would actually think
it, in their words, unedited and unflattering — *"NAD IV drips cost $800 at a clinic.
Two months of capsules for under sixty is a steal."* That column becomes:

- the **objection stack** you must handle,
- the **verbatim phrasing** for your own script,
- the **hook variations** in Phase 6.

Verdict scale: `Very High` / `High` / `Medium` / `Low` — and for anything below High, say
*why it fails* and what would fix it. The failures are more instructive than the wins.

---

## Phase 4 — Brand Adaptation (1:1 Minimal Production Script)

Rewrite beat-for-beat at the same timings for Prime Ingredients, changing **only** what
must change: brand name, product specs, and any claim that doesn't clear
`02-libraries/proof-and-claims.md`.

Three columns: `Time / Beat` · `Spoken line` · `Visual directives & OST`.

Rules:
- Keep the **rhythm and sentence length** of the original. Cadence is a huge part of why a hook holds; rewriting it "better" usually means rewriting it worse.
- Keep the **specific numbers** structure — swap the values, not the specificity.
- Change every claim that doesn't clear the guardrails. Substitute a green-lane claim of equal force; don't just delete it and leave a hole.
- OST is written here, not left to the editor.

*Why "minimal":* every deviation is an untested variable. Adapt minimally first, get a
baseline, then diverge deliberately in Phase 6.

---

## Phase 5 — Modular Templatization & Variable Tagging

Convert the adapted script into a template by replacing every specific with a tagged
variable:

```
[BANNED/RESTRICTED HOOK]
"You know the government made this illegal because it's like [TARGET BIOMARKER] on crack?
 [HIGH AUTHORITY / SCIENTIST], if you know who that is..."

[CREATOR AGE / VULNERABILITY REVEAL]
"I take [HERO MECHANISM]. I've turned back the clock on my [PHYSICAL ASSET] at
 [CREATOR AGE]. I'm [CREATOR AGE]. I turn [NEXT AGE] on [DATE]."

[DEMOGRAPHIC CALLOUT & FOMO CLOSE]
"As a [TARGET DEMOGRAPHIC], if you're not taking [HERO MECHANISM], you're missing
 [AESTHETIC BENEFIT]... give it [TIPPING POINT TIMELINE]."
```

**Standard variable set** — use these names so templates stay interchangeable:

`[HERO MECHANISM]` `[BRAND NAME]` `[TARGET BIOMARKER]` `[HIGH AUTHORITY]`
`[PRIMARY SYMPTOM]` `[SYMPTOM TIME]` `[FALSE CAUSE]` `[FALSE SOLUTION]`
`[CREATOR AGE]` `[TARGET DEMOGRAPHIC]` `[PHYSICAL ASSET]` `[FELT STATE]`
`[DOSE SPEC]` `[PURITY CLAIM]` `[PRICE ANCHOR]` `[TIPPING POINT TIMELINE]` `[CTA MECHANIC]`

*Why:* a templated script generates 20 ads. A written script generates one. This is the
step that converts research into throughput, and it's the step people skip.

---

## Phase 6 — Hook & Angle Diversification

Produce **three** new opens for the same body, one per lane:

1. **Direct Symptom Qualification** — the Linda lane (H05)
2. **First-Person Transformation or Spec/Verification** — the Robert lane (H07 / H06)
3. **Category Contrast / Purity Anchor** — the disqualification lane (H06 / H10)

Each written in full, ready to shoot, with its OST. These are the iterations you batch
in the same session as the original — the marginal cost is four minutes of the creator's
time and it's where most of the account's winners will come from.

---

## Quality gate

A deconstruction is done when you can answer these without re-reading the ad:

- [ ] What is the *one* belief this ad changes in the viewer's head?
- [ ] What does the viewer have to already believe for it to work? (→ awareness level)
- [ ] Which objection does it defeat, and which does it dodge? (The dodge is your opening.)
- [ ] What is the most portable element — the hook, the structure, or the proof asset?
- [ ] What could we not copy even if we wanted to, and what's our substitute?
- [ ] If this ad is working, what is it *actually* doing that our current ads don't?

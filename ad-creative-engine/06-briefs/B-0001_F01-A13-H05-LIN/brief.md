## 3. Hypothesis

> **We believe** Linda at Problem-Aware will respond to the *cost of the lost afternoon*
> angle delivered as a talking-head symptom diagnosis, **because** every competitor in
> the category sells "more energy" as a feeling, and none of them price the loss — four
> hours a day, every day, for years. Quantified loss is a bigger emotional number than
> promised gain.
> **We'll know we're right if** hook rate beats the account median by ≥ 20% and hold-to-50%
> is at or above median, within 4 days and 3× target CPA in spend.
> **If it fails on hook rate**, the arithmetic open is too cold for a 3-second read and
> the angle needs a warmer symptom entry.
> **If it fails after 25%**, the loss framing depresses rather than motivates, and the
> angle is wrong — not the hook.

Method

How I decided what counts as a winner

The Ad Library doesn't publish spend. It publishes time running — and in direct response, that's nearly as good. An ad still live at 60, 90, 120 days is an ad that kept beating the kill threshold. An ad live for four days is a test.

So every copy block here is weighted two ways: how many days it stayed live, and how many separate creatives ran it. A copy that 14 different creatives carried for 122 days is where a competitor put real money.

Five mechanics every long-runner shares

  • Line one is a standalone sentence, then a blank line. Meta truncates at roughly 125 characters behind "See more." Every 60-day ad puts the whole idea before the cut.
  • One idea per ad. Not one ad covering five benefits — five ads covering one benefit each.
  • Emoji are functional, never decorative. ✅ benefit, ❌ competitor fault, ⚡⏳🧠 visual bullets, 👉 the CTA line. Never mid-sentence.
  • Compliance verbs throughout. "Supports", "helps support", "designed to", "studied for." Never cures or treats. NOVOS goes further and discloses "AI-generated performer — not a real person" plus "individual results will vary."
  • Volume is the strategy. Renue runs ~90 distinct copies across six SKUs. Omre runs four hooks across 40+ permutations. They're testing hooks, not polishing one.

Ten structures, ranked by money behind them

02

RUN BY Jinfiniti — longest-running non-creator ad in the set

A sub-move of the ladder, powerful enough to stand alone. You name the excuse the reader has been telling themselves, then reject it on their behalf.

[SYMPTOM] isn't [the normal explanation].
It's not "[the excuse they tell themselves]."
It's not "[the second excuse]."
It's [your mechanism].

03

The Category Callout

RUN BY ProHealth (124d) · Renue · Tru Niagen · NOVOS

Don't argue that you're good. Argue that the category is bad, then prove you're the exception. The buyer already suspects it — you're agreeing with them.

1 ATTACK "Most [category] doesn't live up to the hype"
2 ❌ STACK 3–4 specific, checkable failures
3 ✅ STACK Your mirror-image proof, same order
4 STAKES "If you take this seriously, it should matter"
5 RISK OFF Guarantee that kills the last objection

Borrowed Authority

2 QUOTE Verbatim pull-quote, in quotation marks
3 EXPERT A named MD or PhD attached to the verdict
4 THANKS "Thank you @publication"

08

Segment-Targeted Opening

RUN BY ProHealth (122d, 14 variants — their biggest bet) · Omre · NOVOS · Wonderfeel

The first six words name a person, not a benefit. Same product, different door — and critically, a different landing page behind each door.

  • "For men who care about staying sharp and active past 40, ProHealth NMN isn't hype…" — 122 days
  • "For high performers, even small slips are hard to ignore." — Omre
  • "On GLP-1? By the time skin looks like it has changed…" — NOVOS
  • "Over 25? This is when things start to shift." — Wonderfeel

Omre's execution is the one to steal: one product, four segment hooks, four matching landing pages — /first-order-energy, /mobility-and-independence, /first-order-fitness, /7-reasons-you-start-losing-your-edge. The hook and the page tell the same story. That's how one SKU supports 80 live ads.

09

Offer as News

RUN BY ProHealth (122–124d) · Double Wood (63–133d) · Tru Niagen · Renue

A discount without a reason reads as desperation. A discount with a mechanism reads as news.

ProHealth LongevityImage122 days live14 variants

For men who care about staying sharp and active past 40, ProHealth NMN isn't hype — it's a smarter way to support energy, recovery, and longevity at the source.

The only thing that just changed? The price. We cut it by up to 25% — effective immediately. If you've been waiting for the right time to start — this is it!

Two details worth stealing. "We renegotiated our supply agreement and passed the savings directly to you" gives the discount a cause. And "Same formula. New price." kills the did-they-cheapen-it objection in four words.

Their promo codes are named per angle, never generic — AGELESS for NMN, GLOWDEEPER for serum, BERB10 for berberine. The code carries the positioning and lets you attribute revenue per angle.

Section 03 · Opportunity

Where the whole category is weak

Unoccupied positions. Each of these is nearly free differentiation, because almost nobody in 203 ad copies is doing it.

GapThe evidenceThe opening
First-person testimonialOnly 7.9% of longevity copies use "I / my". The ED advertisers do — and theirs ran 58 days across 19 creatives.Customer-voice copy is nearly vacant here. Renue outsources it entirely to creators.
Time-to-resultAlmost nobody promises a window. The ED cluster says "10 minutes" and runs forever.An honest "week 1 / week 4 / week 12" timeline is unclaimed.
Risk reversalExactly one guarantee in 203 copies — and it ran 124 days.Ship a specific, generous guarantee.
Cost anchoringOnly Omre ("under $2 a day") and NOVOS ("5,000 strawberries").Anchor against the alternative — a latte, a facial, an under-dosed competitor.
Founder storyZero instances across all 22 advertisers.The one thing competitors literally cannot copy.
How to evaluateEveryone leads with the molecule's decline; nobody teaches buyers how to judge one."How to read a label" owns top-of-funnel and pre-frames you as the honest option.

Section 05 · Next

How to deploy this

  1. Build the hook bank before the ad. Write 20 hooks — line one only — across frameworks 01, 02, 03, 06 and 08. Kill on hook performance before writing bodies.
  2. One idea per ad. Five ads about one benefit each beats one ad about five.
  3. Front-load the first 125 characters. That's the "See more" cut.
  4. Match every hook to its own landing page. Segment-hook ads that dump into a generic PDP leak. Omre runs 80 ads off one SKU because each door has its own room.
  5. Test the soft CTA against the hard one. Quiz CTAs hold three of the top longevity spots here. Give it a dedicated cell.
  6. Ship the guarantee. One competitor in 203 copies has real risk reversal and it ran 124 days. Cheapest differentiation available.
  7. Start the creator flywheel. The longest-running ad in this whole set is a whitelisted MD post. Whitelisting a credible practitioner beats brand-voice ads on duration by a wide margin.
  8. Keep the compliance verbs. Supports, helps support, designed to, studied for. Add the FDA disclaimer to structure/function claims and "individual results will vary" to anything testimonial-shaped.

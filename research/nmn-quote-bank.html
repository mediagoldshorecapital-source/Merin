How to work the sheet

  • Writing an ad — filter Emotion + Type, lift the quote as your first line, use the Trigger moment as the opening shot.
  • Briefing a creator — filter by Segment, hand them the trigger moment as the scene and the quote as the line.
  • Building the email flow — sort by Trigger moment. Each one is a send.
  • Finding hero testimonials — filter Score 5 and criteria containing F.
  • Naming the objection — every Objection row is a headline for the page that answers it.

Appendix

Below the bar

These 16 lines are among the loudest in the corpus but they clear only two criteria. That is a finding, not an oversight: the FDA, authority and dose-math arguments are ideological or arithmetic rather than sensory and personal. They have no body in them and no former self to compare against.

Which tells you exactly how to use them — they make excellent hooks and headlines, and terrible testimonials. Put them at the top of an ad, never in a customer's mouth.

Before you run any of it

Claim guardrail

Structure and function only

Several quotes in the source corpus describe reversing Alzheimer's, resolving lung scarring and curing arthritis. Those are excluded from this bank on purpose — running them turns a supplement into an unapproved drug claim.

Everything here stays inside structure/function language: supports cellular energy, supports healthy NAD+ levels, supports healthy aging. Keep the raw review, the date and the customer's consent on file for anything you run as an ad, and screen creator scripts the same way — a claim your creator makes is your claim.

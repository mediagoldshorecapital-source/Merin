### What to notice

- Concept 1 **omits** the ⒝ line entirely. The rule fires on second-person assertions, not on
  every headline. "Done With the 3 PM Crash" names a moment.
- Concept 2 asserts — *your body* + a claim about it — so both versions ship and ⒝ leads.
- The expression direction is explicit in both: flat, faintly irritated, not smiling at camera.
  This is the register that separates this account's winners from stock-photo warmth.
- Every prompt names the label in full, in words, **and** points at the attached reference.
- No capsule count, no per-day cost, no review badge, no percentage discount, in either concept.

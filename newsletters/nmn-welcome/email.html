Why NAD+ matters

NAD+ is a coenzyme your cells rely on to turn food into usable energy. It's quietly at work every second — and it naturally declines as we age. NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide) is the direct precursor your body uses to make more of it, which is why it supports healthy NAD+ levels and everyday cellular energy.

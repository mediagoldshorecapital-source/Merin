The six markers

T a specific time or place

P a physical sensation, not an abstraction

F a comparison to a former self

A a slightly embarrassing admission

L plain unbranded language

W quotable in under twelve words

The verdict rule

Three or more markers earns USE — lines that can carry an ad, a subject line or a headline more or less as written.

### The rules every script here obeys — all measured, none preference

| Rule | The number behind it |
|---|---|
| **Hooks qualify, they do not attract** | Winners CPA $31.90 vs losers $58.09; hook rate correlates **negatively** with ROAS in all seven months |
| **Committed verbs in the ad; hedge in the proof** | Committed 1.41 ROAS vs hedged 0.92 — 53% apart on the same insight |
| **Never "75% OFF" in a headline** | Lower conversions per click in **five months out of five** |
| **Lead the per-bottle anchor** | $29.98 → $16.66. Zero discount codes across 4,770 orders |
| **Talent reads 58–68** | She compares herself to her peer group; G5 shows the audience rejecting the current casting out loud |
| **Plain beats polished** | In this account winners look homemade and losers look like adverts |
| **Video: 20s, hook inside 3, captions burned in** | She watches Reels in a recliner with the TV on and the sound off |
| **Name the moment, never the diagnosis** | §11.4 of the master document. Absolute stop |

### Claim ladder, applied to every line below

`supports / helps maintain` → **claimable in brand voice**
`improves / increases` → **testimonial only, attributed**
`restores / reverses / boosts / treats / cures / prevents` → **rejected, never written**

### The C2 marker

Where a script contains a **second-person assertion about her physiology**, both versions are printed:
**⒜** = as the account currently runs it (measured winner, higher policy exposure)
**⒝** = assertion moved to a disclosure or the third person (lower exposure, untested)
**Recommended split: ⒝ on ~70% of spend, ⒜ on ~30%.** Read conversions per click over four weeks.

### Blockers

🔒 marks a script that **must not be uploaded** until the named operational gate closes. Write it now; hold it.

### Provenance

`OWN-##` = your own Meta ad comments — the only quotes publishable as your social proof, after verifying the commenter ordered.
`RED-###` / `CMP-Q###` / `YT` = Reddit, competitor reviews, YouTube — **mine for phrasing, never attribute as social proof.**
`[I]` = invented line, no customer said it.

### The two supplied frameworks, and where they are used

**Framework 1 — Authority-Led "Mechanism Discovery & Identity Shift"** (6 beats). Best fit: Problem-Aware and Product-Aware, where a mechanism has to land and an identity has to shift. Used in A09, A10, A19, A20, A40, A50.
**Framework 2 — Solution-Aware "Alternative Disqualification"** (7 beats, Zone 3 → Zone 4 → Zone 1). Best fit: Solution-Aware and Product-Aware, where competitors and workarounds must be eliminated first. Used in A29, A30, A39, A49.

**One adaptation both frameworks need for this account.** Both are written for a category where you may assert things about the reader. Yours is not. Every "you have [problem]" beat is rewritten as a question she answers or a disclosure the speaker makes — that is the C2 rule, and it is non-negotiable regardless of what the framework template says.

**Mon 24 Aug** — First real read. Kill everything hitting a kill threshold. Log the reason for each kill — the reasons are the learning, not the kills.

**Tue 25 Aug** — Ideation for Batch 2. Rule: **50% of Batch 2 must be variations of whatever is performing best in Batch 1.** If ad #2 has the best CTR, build five headline-only variants of #2. If nothing is working yet, build 12 new concepts against the same territory quota.

**Wed 26 – Thu 27 AM** — Build 12. Build the remaining 6 Canva templates while you're in there.

**Thu 27 Aug PM — LAUNCH BATCH 2.**

**Fri–Sun** — Watch. Promote any Batch-1 ad that hit all four winner criteria into `[SCALE]` at $100/day.

**End-of-week check:** ≥1 ad under $34 CPA? If zero ads are under $34 after 24 creatives, the problem is probably not creative — check the landing page (93.9% bounce rate) and the offer presentation before building batch 3.

## If you only have time to do three things

1. **Build Format 04 (Timeline) and Format 12 (Bundle Maths) and put them live this week.** They are the direct fix for the AOV decline, and AOV is half of the profit gap.
2. **Ship 12 new statics every Thursday, without exception.** Volume is what pulls the $121 CPM down. One brilliant ad launched slowly loses to twelve decent ads launched weekly.
3. **Log every ad in the tracker with its territory and framework code.** Without it you'll find winning *ads* but never learn which *angle* wins — and the angle is the compounding asset.

## About the brand

Prime Ingredients makes **NMN Complex 1000mg** — a cellular energy supplement. Third-party lab tested. 5,000+ reviews. 90-day money-back guarantee.

The people who buy it are mostly women in their late fifties and sixties who are tired in a way that sleep doesn't fix, and who have been disappointed by supplements before.

## Who you're talking to

Picture one person: **a 62-year-old woman in Phoenix.** She wakes at 6:30 and doesn't feel rested. She has grandchildren she wants to keep up with. Her energy drops off a cliff between 2 and 4 in the afternoon. She has spent money on supplements that did nothing and she is quietly embarrassed about it.

She is sharp. She was probably a teacher or a nurse or ran a small business. **Do not talk down to her.** Talk to her the way you'd talk to a friend across a kitchen table.

## Tone — this matters more than anything else

| Do this | Not this |
|---|---|
| Calm, conversational, like talking to a friend | Excited, salesy, "you guys!" |
| Understated | Enthusiastic |
| Specific and a bit unglamorous | Polished and general |
| Pause when something's hard to say | Fill every second |
| Let yourself stumble a little | Perfect delivery |
| Honest about what didn't work | All upside |

**If it sounds like an advertisement, we can't use it.** The single most valuable thing you can give us is sounding like yourself.

## Setup

- **Film on your phone**, rear camera, **4K if you have it, vertical (9:16)**
- **Use a clip-on microphone** if we've sent one. Audio matters more than picture.
- **Face a window.** Natural light only. Never have a bright window behind you.
- Best times: **9–11 AM or 3–5 PM** for warm light
- Film in a real room in your home — kitchen, porch, living room, garden. It should look lived in. A fruit bowl, a dog bed, a coffee cup — all good.
- Waist-up framing, eyes about a third of the way down the screen
- Phone on a tripod or propped steady. Handheld is fine if it's not shaky.

## Wardrobe

Whatever you'd actually wear on a normal day. Solid colours read best. Avoid busy patterns and avoid pure white.

## What we need you to film

**Please shoot at least 4 takes of everything.** Take 3 is usually the good one.

### Block 1 — Hooks (about 45 minutes)
We'll send you 8–10 short opening lines. Film each one straight to camera, back to back, same spot and same outfit. Each is only about 3 seconds.

**Don't read them.** Read the line, understand the idea, then say it in your own words. Your version is always better than ours.

### Block 2 — Main sections (about 60 minutes)
Three longer sections, roughly 12 seconds each. Same rule: know the idea, say it your way.

### Block 3 — Endings (about 20 minutes)
Three short closing lines about the offer.

### Block 4 — B-roll, no talking (about 45 minutes)
Just film these actions. No dialogue needed.
- Taking two capsules with water (film from 3 different angles)
- The bottle sitting on your kitchen counter in morning light
- Turning the bottle around to read the label
- Pouring coffee
- Walking outside, gardening, watering plants
- Getting up easily from the floor or a low chair
- Opening the delivery box
- Looking at your phone, then tapping the screen
- A wall clock showing 3:00

### Block 5 — Just talk (about 20 minutes)
Prop the phone up and answer these however you like. Take your time.
- What did you notice first?
- What did you think when you ordered it?
- What would you say to a friend who thinks supplements are a waste of money?
- **What didn't change?**

*(That last question is genuinely important to us. Honesty is what makes people trust an ad.)*

## Things we can't use — please avoid

These are legal requirements, not preferences:

- ❌ **Don't tell the viewer what they feel.** Say *"I was exhausted,"* never *"you're exhausted"* or *"at your age you're tired."*
- ❌ **Never mention any disease.** No dementia, Alzheimer's, chronic fatigue, or any illness — not even to say it helps with them.
- ❌ **Don't say it cures, treats, prevents, or fixes anything.** "Supports energy," "supports mental clarity" are fine.
- ❌ **Don't promise timelines we haven't agreed.** If you mention a timeframe, say what actually happened for you.
- ❌ **Don't wear a lab coat, scrubs, or anything implying a medical credential** unless you hold one.
- ❌ **Don't invent results.** Only say what actually happened to you.

- **"1000mg NMN Complex"** — the full name and dose
- **"Third-party lab tested"**
- **"Buy 3 Get 3 Free — $16.66 a bottle"**
- **"90-day money-back guarantee"**

# 06 — Static Ads SOP: 8 Layouts With Exact Build Steps

Statics are the most under-rated asset in this account. Linda is a **slow, careful reader** who has been burned before — she *wants* something she can stop on and study. A static she can read at her own pace outperforms a video she has to keep up with, especially on the tablet (5.11% CVR, your highest-converting device).

Statics are also almost free. One person can build eight in an afternoon.

---

## Universal design rules for this buyer

These are not style preferences. They come directly from who she is.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Headline text ≥ 90px** on a 1080×1350 canvas | She's reading on an iPad at arm's length, possibly without her glasses |
| **Bold sans-serif only** — Inter, Poppins, Montserrat Bold, Archivo | Thin weights and scripts disappear at scroll speed |
| **Contrast ratio 7:1 minimum** — white on near-black, or near-black on cream | Age-related contrast sensitivity is real. Test it by squinting at your phone from 3 feet |
| **One idea per static** | Two competing messages = zero messages |
| **Max 3 text blocks:** headline, support line, offer | More than that and she scrolls |
| **Faces of women 55–68** | She needs to see herself. Models in their 40s actively repel this buyer |
| **Warm natural light, Sunbelt setting** | Kitchen, patio, garden. Never a white studio, never cold blue clinical |
| **Product visible and identifiable** in every static | She's checking it's a real product from a real company |
| **Build natively at 1080×1350 (4:5) and 1080×1920 (9:16)** | Never crop one into the other; text will get cut |
| **Keep the bottom 20% of 9:16 clear** | Stories/Reels UI covers it |
| **No red "SALE" starbursts** | Reads as scam to a burned buyer. Your offer is credible; don't make it look like a spam ad |

## The 8 layouts

### S-1 — Visual Multiples (1 vs. 6)
**Funnel:** MoF/BoF · **Angle:** A8 · **Build: 20 min · Highest priority — build this first**

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  SWAP 6 FOR 1               │  ← headline, 100px bold, dark on cream
├──────────────┬──────────────┤
│  6 bottles   │   1 bottle   │  ← photo, evenly lit, same background
│  cluttered   │   clean      │
│  ~$120/mo    │  $16.66/mo   │  ← price under each, 72px
├──────────────┴──────────────┤
│ 1000mg NMN · 3rd-party tested│  ← support line, 48px
│  BUY 3 GET 3 FREE            │  ← offer bar, white on dark, 80px
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

**Build steps (Canva):**
1. New design → Custom size 1080 × 1350.
2. Background: warm cream `#F5EFE6`.
3. Photograph six generic supplement bottles clustered on the left half of a kitchen counter, and one Prime Ingredients bottle alone on the right half. **Same shot, same light** — do not composite two different photos, it looks fake and she'll notice.
4. Headline top, `#1A1A1A`, Poppins Bold 100px.
5. Vertical divider line, 4px, `#1A1A1A`, 40% opacity.
6. Prices in Poppins Bold 72px under each side. Strike through the $120.
7. Offer bar: full-width rectangle `#1A1A1A`, text white 80px, bottom 18% of the canvas.
8. Export PNG. Duplicate → resize to 1080×1920, move the offer bar up so it clears the bottom 20%.

### S-5 — The Review Screenshot
**Funnel:** BoF · **Build: 10 min · Cheapest asset you can make**

Take a genuine 5-star review with a story, screenshot it clean, and place it on a warm background with the bottle. Add one line of context above and the offer below.

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  From a verified customer:  │
├─────────────────────────────┤
│  ★★★★★                      │
│  "I can actually get on the │  ← the review, verbatim, 60px
│   floor and play with my    │
│   3-year-old grandson       │
│   again."                   │
│         — Darlene M.        │
├─────────────────────────────┤
│ [bottle]  BUY 3 GET 3 FREE  │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

Make one of these for **every** strong review you have. They cost ten minutes each and they're the highest-credibility asset in the account. Build a library of twenty.

### S-6 — The Comparison Table
**Funnel:** MoF · **Angle:** A8/A6 · **Build: 25 min**

| | Typical NMN | Prime Ingredients |
|---|---|---|
| Dose | 150–300mg | **1000mg** |
| Third-party tested | ✗ | **✓** |
| Co-factors included | ✗ | **✓** |
| Cost per day (6-bottle) | $1.00+ | **$0.55** |
| Guarantee | 30 days | **90 days** |

Set your column in bold dark, theirs in grey. Do not name a competitor brand — Meta will reject it and it's legally reckless. "Typical NMN products" is fine.

### S-7 — The Before/After Day
**Funnel:** ToF · **Angle:** A1/A2 · **Build: 20 min**

Split vertically. Top half: a clock at 3:00 PM, muted desaturated grade, a woman slumped in a chair. Bottom half: the same clock, same woman, warm saturated grade, out in the garden. Headline across the join: `SAME 3 PM.`

No physical before/after of a *body* — only a before/after of a *day*. This keeps you clear of Meta's before/after image restrictions and it's the more honest claim anyway.

---

### S-8 — The Single Sentence
**Funnel:** ToF · **Build: 5 min**

Just one enormous sentence on a warm background, with the bottle small in a corner.

> **"It's not that you need more sleep. It's that your cells stopped making energy."**

140px type, 4 lines maximum, nothing else on the canvas. These punch far above their production cost because they read as an idea rather than an ad. Make one for each of your top 6 hooks.

## The static production sprint

**One afternoon, eight assets:**

```
0:00–0:30   Photograph everything you need in one session:
            · 6 bottles clustered + 1 bottle alone (same light) → S-1
            · Coins on a counter → S-3
            · Bottle hero shot on a kitchen counter → S-4, S-6
            · Clock at 3:00 → S-2, S-7
0:30–1:00   Build the master Canva template: brand colours, fonts, offer bar
1:00–3:30   Build S-1 through S-8 at 1080×1350
3:30–4:00   Duplicate all 8 → resize to 1080×1920, reposition, export
```

**Output:** 16 assets (8 concepts × 2 ratios) in four hours.

## Brand colour and type system

Lock these once so every asset looks like it came from the same company. Confirm against the live site before finalising.

```
Ink (headlines, dark bars)   #1A1A1A
Warm ground (backgrounds)    #F5EFE6
Accent (offer bar, ticks)    [pull from tryprimeingredients.com]
Verified green (checkmarks)  #2E7D5B
Alert (strike-through only)  #B23A2E

Headline    Poppins Bold / Archivo Black    90–140px
Body        Inter SemiBold                  48–60px
Offer bar   Poppins Bold, all caps          72–80px
```

Never use more than two typefaces in one asset.

## Static QA checklist

Before any static ships:

- [ ] Readable when I squint at my phone from three feet away
- [ ] Headline is under 10 words
- [ ] Product bottle is visible and identifiable
- [ ] Offer states: **bundle name + per-bottle price + guarantee**
- [ ] Nothing important in the bottom 20% (9:16) or under the CTA button (4:5)
- [ ] All claims are on the verified list in `README.md`
- [ ] Passes the compliance rewrite check in `11-compliance-guardrails.md`
- [ ] Exported at 1080px wide minimum, PNG, under 30MB
- [ ] Filename follows the naming convention from `03-creative-matrix.md`

# 08 — Creative Briefs & Scripts

Production-ready briefs for all five Rings. Hand these to a creator or designer as-is.

> ### ⚠️ Read before writing a single word
> **Every claim below is a placeholder until Prime Ingredients' regulatory reviewer signs it
> off.** Supplements cannot make disease claims. You may agitate a *feeling* she has; you may
> not diagnose, treat, or promise to cure anything.
>
> | ✅ Permitted framing | ❌ Prohibited framing |
> |---|---|
> | "Tired in a way sleep doesn't fix" | "Treats chronic fatigue" |
> | "Supports cellular energy" | "Cures brain fog" |
> | "I feel sharper" (personal experience) | "Prevents dementia / cognitive decline" |
> | "Supports healthy ageing" | "Reverses ageing" |
> | "Most women notice a difference" | "You will feel results in 21 days" |
> | Third-party lab testing (if true) | "Clinically proven" (unless you hold the trial) |
>
> Also prohibited by Meta policy: implying you know a personal attribute about the viewer
> ("at your age…", "because you're overweight…"), and before/after imagery implying a
> medical outcome. Repeated rejections put the ad account itself at risk.

**Substantiation placeholders** — replace with verified sources before publishing:
`[NAD+ decline stat — cite source]`, `[timeframe — cite internal survey or omit]`,
`[press mentions — confirm actual coverage]`, `[lab certification — confirm current COA]`.

## Casting & production standards

| Standard | Requirement | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Creator age | **55–65**, genuinely | Linda detects a 35-year-old playing older instantly, and the ad dies in 2 seconds |
| Look | Real. Minimal makeup, own clothes, own home | Polish reads as "advertisement" |
| Camera | Phone, handheld or propped. **No studio** | Studio quality reduces trust in this demographic |
| Light | Natural, window-lit, daytime | |
| Audio | Phone mic or a clip-on lav. Clean, not produced | |
| Subtitles | **Burned in**, high contrast, ≥ 40px on a 1080-wide frame | Muted viewing + real eyesight constraints |
| Aspect | Shoot **4:5 master**, framed safe for 9:16 crop | 4:5 wins the FB feed, which is 60% of revenue |
| Length | R1 20–45s · R2 45s–10min (VSL) · R3 45–90s | |
| Delivery | Raw + graded, plus **3 alternate hook cuts per asset** | Hook rotation is the cheapest revival move (file 07 §5) |

# RING 1 — First Hello: Energy

**Job:** stop a cold scroll with a sensation she felt this week.
**Format:** UGC video, 20–45s. **Angle:** `3PMCRASH`. **Persona:** Linda, 58–62.
**Metric:** hook rate ≥ 25%, Amount Spent. **Not judged on ROAS.**

### Script — `R1_3PMCRASH_UGC_LINDA62_[SCENE]_v1`

> **[0–3s · HOOK — hold on her face, no product, no branding]**
> "It's three o'clock and I'm already done for the day."
>
> **[3–8s · AGITATE]**
> "Not sleepy-tired. The kind where you've had eight hours and you still feel like you're
> walking through water."
>
> **[8–15s · REFRAME — this is the pivot that earns the click]**
> "I thought that was just… turning sixty. It isn't. There's a molecule your cells run on
> called NAD+, and by the time you're my age you've got a fraction of what you had at
> thirty. `[cite source]`"
>
> **[15–28s · SOLUTION, understated]**
> "So I started taking NMN — it's what your body uses to make NAD+. One in the morning with
> coffee. `[timeframe]` I noticed I wasn't hitting that wall in the afternoon anymore. I got
> through dinner without needing to sit down first."
>
> **[28–40s · PROOF + SOFT CTA]**
> "I use Prime Ingredients because it's 1000mg and it's third-party tested — I've been
> burned before. `[confirm COA]`"
> "If your afternoons feel like mine did, it's worth reading about."

**On-screen text beats:** `3:00 PM` → `It's not just ageing` → `NAD+` → `Prime Ingredients NMN Complex 1000mg`

### The three environments (the 3:2:2 pack)

| | Scene | Wardrobe | Framing | Notes |
|---|-------|----------|---------|-------|
| **A** | Bright kitchen, morning coffee | Light cardigan | Propped, mid-shot | Warm, aspirational |
| **B** | **Car after a grocery run** | Jacket, seatbelt on | Handheld selfie | ⭐ Usually the winner. Highest relatability — she's tired *right now* |
| **C** | Couch, late afternoon light | Loungewear | Propped, slightly low | Most literal agitation |

**Same script, three environments. Do not rewrite the script between takes.**

### Angle variants (backlog — build after the launch asset proves out)
- `BRAINFOG` — open: *"I stood in my own kitchen and couldn't remember why I'd walked in."*
- `GRANDKIDS` — open: *"My granddaughter asked me to get down on the floor and play. I said maybe later. I say maybe later a lot now."*

# RING 2 — Second Hello: Authority / Clarity

**Job:** the rational hello, for the woman who needs a mechanism before she'll believe.
**Format:** founder or expert explainer / VSL. **Angle:** `BRAINFOG`.
**Metric:** hold rate ≥ 20%, Amount Spent.

### 2a. Founder story (45–90s) — `R2_SKEPTIC_FOUNDER_v1`

> **[0–5s · HOOK — tension and disclosed motive, never a callout]**
> "We nearly didn't launch this product. Our first batch came back from the lab and it
> wasn't what the supplier said it was."
>
> **[5–20s]** "I'd watched my mother describe it as fog. She'd lose a word mid-sentence and
> laugh it off, and I could see it frightened her."
>
> **[20–45s]** "So I looked into NAD+ — the molecule every cell uses to make energy, including
> the ones in your brain. It falls off sharply with age. `[cite]` NMN is the precursor your
> body actually uses to make it."
>
> **[45–70s]** "The problem is the supplement industry. Most 'NMN' on the market is
> under-dosed or isn't NMN at all. That's why we test every single batch with a third-party
> lab and publish the certificate. `[confirm]`"
>
> **[70–90s]** "1000mg. Tested. If it doesn't do anything for you in 30 days, send it back.
> That's the whole offer."

**Why this works on Linda:** she has been burned by cheap supplements and reads labels. A
disclosed failure is the strongest trust signal you own — far stronger than a claim.

### 2b. VSL (3–10 min) — `R2_BRAINFOG_VSL_v1`

The highest-ceiling format for 50+. Structure:

| Stage | Duration | Content | Rule |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| **Unaware** | 0:00–1:30 | A story about a moment of forgetting. No product, no science, no brand | Nothing that signals "ad" |
| **Problem-aware** | 1:30–4:00 | Name the pattern. "It isn't memory, it's cellular energy." Introduce NAD+ | Still no product |
| **Solution-aware** | 4:00–6:30 | Why NMN specifically. Why dose matters. Why most products fail | Category, not brand |
| **Product** | **6:30+** | Prime Ingredients. Dose, testing, guarantee, bundle | ⚠️ **Never earlier** |
| **Close** | Final 60s | Offer, guarantee, single clear CTA | |

> **The delayed reveal is the mechanism.** Naming the product early converts the video from
> a story into an advertisement and she leaves. Intent has to be built before the product
> appears.

# RING 3 — Third Hello: Social Proof

**Job:** "women like me are already doing this."
**Format:** 3-woman testimonial mashup, 45–90s. **Angle:** `3PMCRASH` (launch).
**Metric:** hold rate, Amount Spent.

### Structure — `R3_3PMCRASH_TESTIMONIAL_MASHUP_v1`

| Time | Speaker | Beat | Sample line |
|------|---------|------|-------------|
| 0–5s | Woman 1 (58) | **Hook — skepticism first** | "I'll be honest, I thought this was going to be another bottle in the cupboard." |
| 5–20s | Woman 1 | Her before/after, in her own words | "I used to plan my day around the afternoon. Now I don't think about it." |
| 20–40s | Woman 2 (63) | Different benefit — clarity | "Names. I'd lost people's names. That's the thing that's come back." |
| 40–60s | Woman 3 (55) | Different benefit — activity | "We do the walk with the dog again. All of it, not half of it." |
| 60–75s | All three, cut fast | Rapid-fire proof | "Six weeks." / "About a month." / "I noticed before my husband did." |
| 75–90s | Card | Offer + guarantee | Buy 3 Get 3 Free · 30-day money-back |

**Casting:** three visibly different women — different ages within 48–65, different regions,
different homes. If they look like the same person, Meta will treat this as one asset.

**Compliance:** these must be **real customers with real, documented experiences and signed
releases.** Fabricated testimonials are a legal exposure that dwarfs any ad-account risk.
Add an on-screen disclaimer: *"Individual results vary."*

# RING 4 — First Close: Direct Offer

**Job:** convert the woman the videos already warmed up.
**Format:** high-contrast static. **Angle:** `SKEPTIC`. **Metric:** Cost per Purchase ≤ $25.

### Design brief — `R4_SKEPTIC_STATIC_BUNDLE_LABCERT_v1`

**Canvas:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Brand palette: deep forest `#2E3D31`, warm caramel `#A9805A`,
sand `#EFE9E0`, ivory `#FBF8F3`.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  BUY 3 · GET 3 FREE          ← 90px bold, caramel on forest
│  ─────────────────────────           │
│                                      │
│     [6 bottles, hero shot]           │  ← physically show all six.
│                                      │     Quantity does the selling.
│  1000mg NMN Complex                  │  ← 48px
│  6 months. One decision.             │  ← 40px — the commitment frame
│                                      │
│  ✓ 3rd-party lab tested [confirm]    │
│  ✓ 30-day money-back guarantee       │
│  ✓ Made in the USA [confirm]         │
│                                      │
│  [ SHOP THE BUNDLE ]                 │  ← high-contrast button
│                                      │
│  [press badges — confirm actual]     │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Non-negotiables:**
- Show **all six bottles physically.** The perceived value is quantity, not percentage
- Minimum type size **40px**. This audience's eyesight is a design constraint
- Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 on every text element
- Trust marks visible **without zooming**
- Price on the creative **matches the landing page exactly** — any mismatch destroys trust
  with a buyer whose core objection is being deceived

**Primary text (2 options for the 3:2:2):**
- *Rational:* "1000mg NMN Complex. Third-party tested, every batch. Buy 3, get 3 free — a
  six-month supply, because that's how long a real routine takes. 30-day money-back
  guarantee."
- *Emotional:* "You've bought supplements before that did nothing. So had we. That's why we
  publish the lab results and give you 30 days to send it back. Buy 3, get 3 free."

**Headlines:** "Buy 3, Get 3 Free — 1000mg NMN" · "Third-Party Tested NMN Complex"

# RING 5 — Second Close: Objection Handling

**Job:** neutralise the last doubt.
**Format:** static review card, or long-form copy static. **Angle:** `SKEPTIC`.
**Metric:** Cost per Purchase ≤ $25.

### 5a. Review static — `R5_SKEPTIC_STATIC_REVIEW_v1`

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ★★★★★                              │
│                                      │
│  "I tried resveratrol for a year     │  ← the actual objection,
│   and felt nothing. I assumed        │     in a customer's words
│   this would be the same.            │
│   It wasn't."                        │
│                                      │
│  — [Real customer], [Age], [State]   │  ← ⚠️ must be a real,
│    Verified Buyer                    │     documented review
│                                      │
│  [product shot]  1000mg NMN Complex  │
│                  Buy 3 · Get 3 Free  │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5b. Long-form copy static — the highest spend-ceiling asset you can make

A single simple image plus 150–400 words of primary text. Cheap to produce, performs
exceptionally in the Facebook feed for 50+, and scales to very large spend.

> **[Primary text — 300 words]**
>
> I'd bought three different "anti-ageing" supplements before this one.
>
> The first was a resveratrol that a friend swore by. Took it for eleven months. Nothing. The
> second came in a beautiful jar and turned out to be mostly rice flour — I only found out
> because my daughter looked up the company. The third I don't want to talk about.
>
> So when I say I was skeptical about NMN, I mean I had earned it.
>
> What changed my mind wasn't the marketing. It was that Prime Ingredients publishes the
> third-party lab certificate for every batch. `[confirm]` You can read what's actually in
> the bottle. After the rice flour, that mattered more to me than any promise.
>
> Here's what I understand about it now. Your cells run on something called NAD+. It's not
> optional — it's what turns food into usable energy, in every cell you have, including the
> ones in your brain. It declines substantially with age. `[cite]` NMN is the raw material
> your body uses to make more of it.
>
> That's the whole mechanism. No miracle, no reversal, no nonsense.
>
> `[timeframe]`, the afternoon wall I'd been scheduling my life around stopped showing up. I
> noticed it on a Tuesday because I'd made plans for 4 PM without thinking twice about it.
>
> It's 1000mg. It's tested. There's a 30-day money-back guarantee, which after what I've
> been through is the only reason I was willing to try a fourth one.
>
> They do a Buy 3, Get 3 Free — which is what I'd suggest, because a month isn't long enough
> to tell.

**Image:** one plain, warm photograph — bottle on a kitchen counter, morning light. Not a
graphic. The copy is the ad; the image just has to feel real.

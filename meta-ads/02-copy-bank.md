## Framework 5 — The Testimonial Screenshot

*She is skeptical of brands, not of women like her. Take a real review, in her exact language, and make it the whole ad.*

**Rules — these are legal, not stylistic:**
- The review must be **real and verbatim** from your 5,128 reviews.
- Attribute it: first name + last initial + city (e.g. *Darlene M., Tucson AZ*). Verified-buyer badge if you have it.
- **Never write a review that no customer wrote.** Fabricated testimonials are unlawful under the FTC's fake-review rule, with per-violation civil penalties. See `08-compliance.md`.
- No edits except trimming — and mark trims with an ellipsis.

**Review-mining brief for whoever pulls them:** search your Shopify/Judge.me export for reviews containing these exact strings, because they're her words, not marketing's:

```
"3 o'clock"        "afternoon"        "nap"           "brain fog"
"foggy"            "sharp"            "keep up"       "grandkids"
"grandchildren"    "myself again"     "10 years"      "skeptical"
"didn't expect"    "wasted money"     "finally"       "sleep doesn't"
```

**Pick reviews with these shapes (in priority order):**
1. Skeptic-converted — *"I've wasted so much money on supplements. I honestly expected nothing…"*
2. Specific-moment — *"I got down on the floor with my granddaughter and got back up without thinking about it."*
3. Time-anchored — *"Around week three I noticed I wasn't looking for the couch at 3."*
4. Third-party-noticed — *"My husband asked what I was doing differently."*

**Overlay headline above the screenshot** (this is what stops the scroll — the review alone is not enough):
- **"This is the review that made me try it."**
- **"5,128 women wrote reviews. This one says it best."**
- **"Read this one twice."**
- **"She was skeptical too."**

## Words to use / words to ban

**Use — her vocabulary, taken from the report and reviews:**
tired in a way sleep doesn't fix · 3 o'clock · afternoon · foggy · sharp · keep up · the floor · myself again · finally · I was skeptical · lab tested · money back · grandkids

**Ban — brand vocabulary she doesn't trust:**
"unlock" · "biohack" · "optimize" · "game-changer" · "revolutionary" · "supercharge" · "secret" · "doctors hate" · "miracle" · "anti-aging breakthrough" · "cure" · "treat" · "reverse aging" · any emoji fireworks 🎉🔥💥

**Ban outright (compliance):** anything naming a disease (dementia, Alzheimer's, menopause as a condition to treat), anything promising a cure, and any claim you cannot support with a document in your files.

# 03 — Creative System SOP

> **The premise:** in 2026 the creative *is* the targeting. Andromeda scans your asset
> during a ~200ms retrieval phase and matches it to a cluster of users in vector space. You
> are no longer choosing an audience — you are producing an artefact that summons one.
>
> This changes what "creative testing" means. You are not looking for the prettiest ad. You
> are looking for **assets that reach different vector clusters.**

---

## 1. The Concept — the real unit of work

A Concept is not an ad. A Concept is:

```
CONCEPT = PERSONA  ×  ANGLE  ×  FORMAT  ×  OFFER
```

Meta's GEM model classifies your creative multimodally and assigns an **Entity ID**. Two
ads that share an Entity ID are treated as duplicates: they get served to *the same narrow
pool*, they drive frequency up, and they unlock zero incremental reach. You paid twice for
one audience.

**Therefore the governing rule of all creative production:**

> ### 🔴 Every new asset must change **Angle + Persona + Format simultaneously.**
> Changing only the thumbnail, only the headline, or only the music produces a duplicate
> Entity ID and wastes the slot.

---

## 2. The Olympic Rings — five jobs, permanently

You need exactly five *jobs* filled. Ring count never changes; the assets filling them
rotate constantly.

| Ring | Job | Traffic | Format | Success metric |
|:---:|-----|---------|--------|----------------|
| **1** | First Hello — Energy | Cold | UGC video, 20–45s | Amount Spent, hook rate ≥ 25% |
| **2** | Second Hello — Clarity/Authority | Cold | Founder or expert explainer / VSL | Amount Spent, hold rate ≥ 20% |
| **3** | Third Hello — Social Proof | Cold | Multi-person testimonial mashup | Amount Spent, hold rate |
| **4** | First Close — Direct Offer | Warm | Static, high contrast | Cost per Purchase ≤ $25 |
| **5** | Second Close — Objection Handling | Warm | Static review / long-form copy static | Cost per Purchase ≤ $25 |

**Rings 1–3 must say hello in genuinely different ways.** If all three are a 55-year-old
woman talking to camera in her kitchen, you have one Ring, filmed three times, and Meta
will treat it as one.

**Rings 4–5 are judged on CPA. Rings 1–3 are not.** This is the funnel-synergy rule from
`00-doctrine.md §4` made operational. A Ring 1 video with a 0.9x ad-level ROAS and 35% of
campaign spend is doing its job perfectly.

## 3. The Concept Matrix — Prime Ingredients

Five Rings × four Angles. This is your production map. Fill a cell, you have a concept.

|  | `3PMCRASH` | `BRAINFOG` | `GRANDKIDS` | `SKEPTIC` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** Energy hello | ★ **Launch** — UGC, F58, kitchen, 3 PM slump | UGC, F62, desk, forgot a name | UGC, F60, park, can't keep up | — |
| **R2** Authority hello | Founder: why NAD+ falls 50% by 40 | ★ **Launch** — Founder/expert on NAD+ and mental sharpness | — | Founder: why we third-party test every batch |
| **R3** Social proof hello | ★ **Launch** — 3-woman mashup, energy | 3-woman mashup, clarity | 3-woman mashup, grandkids | Mashup of "I was skeptical too" openers |
| **R4** Direct offer close | Static: B3G3, "beat the 3 PM crash" | Static: B3G3, clarity framing | — | ★ **Launch** — Static: B3G3 + lab cert + Forbes/ELLE badges |
| **R5** Objection close | Static review: "all-day energy, no crash" | Static review: "I remember names again" | — | ★ **Launch** — Static: "I tried resveratrol, it did nothing. This is different" |

★ = the five launch assets. Everything else is your production backlog, in priority order.

**Cells left blank are deliberate.** `GRANDKIDS` doesn't work as a static close (it's a
narrative angle, it needs time), and `SKEPTIC` doesn't work as a first hello (you can't
handle an objection before you've made a claim). Don't fill them for symmetry.

## 4. Entity-ID diversification checklist

Before any asset enters an ad set, it must pass. **Three or more boxes ticked, and at least
one from each block.**

**Block A — Semantic (what it says)**
- [ ] Different Angle from every live asset in this campaign
- [ ] Different opening 10 words
- [ ] Different core promise (energy vs. clarity vs. mobility vs. trust)
- [ ] Different objection addressed

**Block B — Visual (what it looks like)**
- [ ] Different on-camera person
- [ ] Different location/setting
- [ ] Different wardrobe and colour palette
- [ ] Different framing (handheld selfie vs. tripod mid-shot vs. b-roll-led)
- [ ] Different first frame — assume Meta reads frame 1 hardest

**Block C — Structural (how it's built)**
- [ ] Different format (UGC / VSL / founder / testimonial / static / advertorial)
- [ ] Different length band (< 20s / 20–45s / 45–90s / 90s+)
- [ ] Different audio bed (voice-only / music / ambient)
- [ ] Different aspect ratio emphasis (4:5 feed-first vs. 9:16 reels-first)

> **The 3-second test.** Play the first 3 seconds of your new asset next to the first 3
> seconds of each live asset, muted. If a stranger could not instantly tell them apart,
> Meta can't either. Reshoot.

**What does *not* count as differentiation:**

❌ New thumbnail · ❌ New headline on the same video · ❌ New music on the same edit ·
❌ Same creator, same room, different sentence · ❌ Same static, different colour ·
❌ AI-upscaled version of an existing asset

## 5. The 3:2:2 Flexible Ad

Every test ad set contains **exactly one ad**, and that ad is a Flexible Ad built 3:2:2.

```
3 creatives  ×  2 primary texts  ×  2 headlines  =  12 combinations
                                                     in ONE data bucket
```

**Why one bucket matters:** twelve separate ads would split your conversion data twelve
ways and none would ever leave learning. One Flexible Ad keeps conversion density
concentrated while still letting Meta find the best combination.

### The rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Never mix media types** | 3 videos, or 3 statics. Never 2 videos + 1 static — Meta cannot compare them fairly and the static will eat the budget |
| **Same Ring, same Angle** | All 3 creatives fill the *same* job. The variable under test is *visual execution*, not message |
| **The script is fixed** | This is Becker's core insight: once a script wins, stop rewriting it. Re-shoot it in new environments |
| **2 texts = 1 rational + 1 emotional** | See below |
| **2 headlines = 1 benefit + 1 authority** | See below |
| **One 3:2:2 per test ad set** | Two would recreate the fragmentation you're avoiding |

### Worked example — Ring 1, `3PMCRASH`

**3 creatives** (same script, three environments — the "Flex" method):
- `A` — making morning coffee in a bright kitchen
- `B` — sitting in the car after a grocery run *(highest relatability, usually wins)*
- `C` — on the couch, visibly exhausted, late afternoon light

**2 primary texts:**
- *Rational:* "By age 40, you've lost 50% of your NAD+. Prime Ingredients NMN Complex
  1000mg restores your cellular energy — most women feel it inside 21 days."
- *Emotional:* "Tired of waking up exhausted? Women over 50 are reclaiming their mornings
  and beating the 3 PM crash with one daily ritual."

**2 headlines:**
- *Benefit:* "Restore Your Cellular Energy"
- *Authority:* "The #1 Rated NAD+ Booster for Women Over 50"

### Build path
`Ad level → Ad setup → Flexible ad → add up to 10 media → Add text options (2) → Add headline options (2)`

Meta occasionally renames this control between releases. If you don't see "Flexible ad",
look for the option to add multiple media and multiple text options within a single ad —
that is the same feature.

## 6. Format doctrine for a 48–65 audience

This demographic behaves differently from the 25–40 audience most Meta advice is written
for. These four formats are specifically indicated.

### 6.1 VSL (Video Sales Letter) — highest ceiling
- 3–10 minutes. Yes, really — it crushes for 50+
- Structure: **unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product**
- **Delay the product reveal.** Do not name NMN in the first 60 seconds. Introduce it only
  once intent is high
- Best for Ring 2

### 6.2 Long-form copy statics — highest spend ceiling per dollar of production
- A single image + 150–400 words of storytelling primary text
- Performs exceptionally in the Facebook feed for 50+ (where 60% of your revenue is)
- Cheap to produce, and they scale to very large spend
- Best for Ring 5

### 6.3 Founder story — highest trust
- Older buyers trust transparency and disclosed motive
- Hook on **tension and risk**: why the product was made, what was at stake, what went
  wrong first
- Best for Ring 2, and the natural home for the `SKEPTIC` angle

### 6.4 UGC — highest volume, fastest iteration
- Creators cast **55–65**, not 35 playing older. Linda detects the fake instantly
- Shot on a phone, natural light, no studio polish
- Best for Rings 1 and 3

## 7. The hook library

The first 3 seconds decide everything. Hook rate ≥ 25% is the gate.

**Never open with a callout.** "Attention women over 50" announces that this is an ad and
she scrolls.

| Hook type | Template | Prime Ingredients example |
|-----------|----------|---------------------------|
| **Real-time symptom** | *"It's [time] and I'm already [symptom]."* | "It's 3 o'clock and I'm already done for the day." |
| **Reframe** | *"[Symptom]? It's not [assumed cause]."* | "Feeling more tired as you age? It's not just aging." |
| **Confession** | *"I didn't tell anyone this for [time]."* | "I didn't tell my daughter I'd started forgetting names." |
| **Specific number** | *"[Stat] — and nobody tells you."* | "By 40 you've lost half your NAD+. Nobody tells you that." |
| **Contrarian** | *"Stop [common advice]. Do this instead."* | "Stop drinking more coffee. It's making the 3 PM crash worse." |
| **Objection-first** | *"I'd been burned by [category] before."* | "I'd been burned by supplements before. Three times." |
| **Peer proof** | *"Women in [place] are doing [thing]."* | "Women in Arizona are quietly beating the afternoon crash." |

### Trial Reel pre-testing (free hook validation)
Before you spend a dollar on a hook, validate it organically:

1. Cut 10–20 hook variations, **CTA removed**
2. Post as Instagram **Trial Reels** (shown to non-followers only)
3. Read retention at 3s and 10s
4. Only hooks in the top quartile earn paid budget

This is the single cheapest way to raise creative hit rate, and almost nobody does it.

## 8. Production cadence

| Spend | Quota | Cadence | Shoot plan |
|-------|-------|---------|------------|
| $4.5k/mo | 5 ads | ~1 new concept/wk | 1 creator day/month, 2 angles |
| $9k/mo | 9 ads | 2 new concepts/wk | 2 creator days/month, 3 angles + 1 founder |
| $22.5k/mo | 23 ads | 5–6/wk | Weekly creator day, rotating cast of 4+ |
| $45k/mo | 45 ads | 10+/wk | Dedicated creative pod, standing roster |

**Batch efficiently:** one creator day should produce 3 Rings × 3 environments = 9 assets,
not 1. Shoot the same winning script in all three environments in a single session — that's
one 3:2:2 built in an afternoon.

### Weekly creative meeting agenda (30 min)
1. Which Ring is weakest this week? *(from the Monday Review)*
2. Which cell of the Concept Matrix fills it?
3. Does the proposed asset pass the Entity-ID checklist? *(§4)*
4. Assign: script owner, creator, shoot date, ready-by date
5. Confirm next Thursday's launch slot has assets ready

## 9. Fatigue: what it is and what to do

**Every ad fatigues. This is physics, not failure.**

### Correct diagnosis first

| Signal pattern | Diagnosis | Action |
|----------------|-----------|--------|
| Frequency ↑, CPM flat, CPA ↑ | **Genuine fatigue** — pool exhausted | Rotate hook (below) |
| Frequency ↓, spend ↑, CPA ↑ slightly | **Graduation** — the ad has left the warm pool and is prospecting higher up the funnel | ✅ **Do nothing.** This is success mid-flight |
| Frequency flat, CPM ↑, CPA ↑ | **External** — auction pressure, seasonality | Do nothing to creative |
| Everything flat, CPA ↑ | Check landing page, checkout, stock, site speed | Not an ads problem |

> The second row is the one that costs people the most money. A falling frequency with
> rising spend looks like a dying ad and is exactly the opposite.

### The revival ladder — try in this order, never skip to the bottom

1. **Rotate the hook.** Keep the body of the winning video, cut 3 new first-5-seconds.
   Cheapest, fastest, highest hit rate.
2. **Re-shoot the same script in a new environment.** New Entity ID, proven script.
3. **Translate format.** Turn a winning VSL script into a long-form static advertorial. This
   unlocks an entirely different placement/format audience from an asset you already own.
4. **Retire, and replace the Ring** with a new concept from the Matrix.

**Never** turn a fatigued winner fully off before its replacement has been proven in a test
ad set. You will take the CPA hit twice.

## 10. Creative QA — before anything goes live

- [ ] Passes the Entity-ID checklist (§4)
- [ ] Hook contains no broad callout
- [ ] Subtitles burned in (a large share of this audience watches muted, and burned-in beats
      auto-captions for legibility)
- [ ] Text legible on a **phone at arm's length** — this audience's eyesight is a real design
      constraint, not a metaphor. Minimum ~40px type on a 1080-wide asset
- [ ] Checked on **iPad** — the highest-converting device at 5.11%
- [ ] 4:5 and 9:16 both render without cropping the message
- [ ] Claims are compliant: no disease claims, no "cures", no before/after implying medical
      outcomes, no personal-attribute targeting language ("you're overweight", "at your age")
- [ ] Offer and price on the creative **match the landing page exactly**
- [ ] Named per the convention in file 01 §7

> ⚠️ **Compliance is a hard gate for supplements.** Meta rejects health claims aggressively
> and repeated rejections put the ad account at risk. Agitate a *feeling* ("tired in a way
> sleep doesn't fix"), never diagnose a *condition*. Never imply treatment of menopause,
> dementia, or any medical condition.

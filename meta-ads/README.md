## The 60-second version

**Who:** Linda, 62, Phoenix/Tucson/Las Vegas/Houston/Miami. iPhone and iPad. Tired in a way sleep doesn't fix. Terrified of cognitive decline. Been burned by supplements before. Buys 6 bottles, not 1.

**What we sell her:** Not a supplement — a 3-6 month commitment to feeling like herself again, at $16.66/bottle via Buy 3 Get 3 Free.

**How the ad works:** One idea, readable in one second, at arm's length, on a phone. Headline does 80% of the work. Every single ad carries social proof. 4:5 and 9:16 only — never design for desktop.

**The number we're moving:** Blended CPA from **$39.68 → $30** and AOV from **$55.78 → $62**. That single swing turns a **−$12,926/month loss into roughly +$18,600/month profit** at today's spend level. See `01-strategy-and-brief.md` for the full arithmetic.

## Before you start — 3 things to confirm

The brand site (`tryprimeingredients.com`) is blocked by this environment's network proxy, so these came from the intelligence report and account data rather than the live product page. Confirm them once, then this playbook is fully locked:

1. **Offer & price** — Buy 3 Get 3 Free at ~$100–106 ($16.66/bottle), anchor $29.98/bottle. Still current?
2. **Trust assets** — exact review count (report says 5,128), the Forbes/ELLE placements, guarantee length (30/60/90 day), and whether "64% of NMN products fail lab tests" has a citable source on file.
3. **Product facts** — mg per serving, capsules per bottle, third-party testing certificate, and whether the label is the one shown in current creative.

Anywhere you see `[CONFIRM]` in these files, that's a field waiting on one of the above.

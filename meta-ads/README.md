## The one-paragraph version

Meta's algorithm no longer needs you to pick audiences — **the creative is the targeting.** Your job is to manufacture a high volume of *distinct* creative concepts, each one aimed at a specific person having a specific bad moment (waking up exhausted at 6:30 AM, the 2–4 PM crash, blanking on a name), prove the claim visually, remove the price objection with bundle math, and neutralize supplement scepticism with third-party proof. Then you measure four numbers per creative — hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA — and let the data, not taste, decide what lives.

---

## The 7-day loop (this is the whole job)

```
MON   Research + mine comments/reviews (2 hrs)  →  no launches today, it is the worst sales day
TUE   Write concepts + scripts (3 hrs)
WED   Produce / film / design (4 hrs)
THU   QA + LAUNCH new creatives (1 hr)  ←  Thu–Sat are the highest-order days
FRI   Leave it alone. Do not touch.
SAT   Leave it alone. Highest order day of the week.
SUN   Leave it alone.
MON   Score last week's cohort against the scorecard → kill / iterate / scale
```

**Non-negotiables:** every new creative gets 7 full days and ~1× AOV per day of forced spend before you judge it. Never launch on a Monday. Never kill on day 2.

## 30-day quick start (if you only do one thing)

1. Read `11-compliance-guardrails.md` and `01-foundation.md`. (1 hour)
2. Run the Phase 1 research sprint in `02-research-sop.md` once. (4 hours)
3. Pick **6 concepts** from `03-creative-matrix.md` — 3 top-of-funnel, 2 middle, 1 bottom.
4. Film them using scripts 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 and static layout S-1 from `05` and `06`.
5. Launch with the exact campaign structure in `08-launch-and-testing-sop.md`.
6. Score on day 8 using `templates/creative-tracker.csv` and the thresholds in `09`.
7. Kill the bottom half, make 3 hook variants of the top performer, relaunch Thursday.

That loop, repeated, is the entire business.

## Facts to verify before you spend a dollar

The source documents disagree on one number and several claims need substantiation on file. **Confirm these against the live site and your supplier paperwork before they appear in an ad:**

| Item | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "Buy 3 Get 3 Free = $58" | ⚠️ **Conflicts.** The customer-intelligence data shows B3G3 = 6 bottles at **~$100–$106** (~$16.66/bottle). $58 is close to blended AOV, not the B3G3 price. | Use **"6 bottles, under $100, $16.66/bottle, 55¢/day."** Never write "$58" for B3G3. |
| "64% of NMN products fail lab tests" | Needs a citable source | Keep the study/report on file; link it on the landing page |
| "Forbes / ELLE" badges | Needs proof of mention | Screenshot + URL on file before use |
| "5,128 reviews / 500K+ customers" | Refresh monthly | Pull live number the day you build the creative |
| "90-day money-back guarantee" | Must match site terms exactly | Confirm wording with the policy page |
| "NAD+ drops up to 50% after 50" | Structure-function claim | Cite the study in your substantiation file |
| Bottle = 30-day supply | Implied by 55¢/day math | Confirm capsule count and serving size |

Accurate claims are not a legal formality here — this buyer has been burned before and **reads every line.** A number she can catch as wrong costs you the sale.

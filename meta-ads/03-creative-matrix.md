# 03 — The Creative Matrix: How Every Concept Gets Generated

You never "think of an ad." You **assemble** one from four slots:

```
ANGLE  ×  HOOK  ×  FORMAT  ×  AVATAR   =   CONCEPT
```

Fill the four slots and a concept exists. This is how a beginner produces 12 distinct concepts in an afternoon instead of staring at a wall.

### The angle you are probably under-using

**A4 (brain fog / cognitive fear)** is emotionally the most powerful angle in this entire dataset and it is almost certainly under-produced. "Am I losing my mind?" is described in the intelligence report as *the most visceral fear for anyone 55+.* Handle it with care — never imply diagnosis or disease (see `11-compliance-guardrails.md`) — but do not avoid it. Frame it as **clarity and sharpness**, never as dementia prevention.

## Slot 2 — The Hook (the first 3 seconds)

Ten hook *archetypes*. The written hooks themselves live in `04-hook-library.md`; this is the taxonomy so you can make sure you're testing variety, not ten versions of the same shape.

| # | Archetype | Shape | Example shell |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Listicle / mythbust** | "N truths about X that nobody tells you" | "4 truths about waking up exhausted after 8 hours of sleep." |
| H2 | **Swap this for that** | "I swapped X for Y — here's what happened" | "I swapped my 3 PM coffee for one capsule." |
| H3 | **So you don't have to** | "I did X for N days so you don't have to" | "I took 1000mg NMN every day for 60 days so you don't have to." |
| H4 | **Counter-intuitive question** | "What's the #1 reason X? Hint: it's not Y" | "…hint: it's not your mattress." |
| H5 | **Red flags / warning** | "N red flags of [hidden problem]" | "3 red flags your afternoon crash isn't normal ageing." |
| H6 | **Accidental discovery** | "[Mechanism] that made [authority] dump [old thing] happened by accident" | — |
| H7 | **Confession / taboo** | A vulnerable admission, said plainly to camera | "I stopped sitting on the floor with my grandson. I'll tell you why." |
| H8 | **Visual action** | No words needed — an action that stops the thumb | Sweeping 6 bottles off a counter |
| H9 | **Us vs. them comparison** | Two things side by side, instantly | 1 bottle vs. a pile of 6 |
| H10 | **Authority statement** | Credible person, natural setting, plain claim | Pharmacist at a counter with a lab report |

**Rule:** in any batch of 6 concepts, use **at least 4 different archetypes.** Six variations of H1 is not a test — it's one test run six times.

### What makes a hook *targeted*, not just loud

The hook's job is to make the *wrong* person scroll past and the *right* person freeze. Include at least one of:

- **An age or life-stage marker** — "after 60," "since menopause," "my grandson" *(as narrative, not as an assertion about the viewer — see compliance)*
- **A specific hour** — "3 PM," "6:30 in the morning"
- **A specific physical sensation** — "heavy," "foggy," "like wading through mud"
- **A named false cause she's already blamed** — "the mattress," "the caffeine," "just getting older"

That last one is the highest-leverage trick available. She has *already privately blamed something.* Naming her wrong theory and dismissing it in the same breath creates an unclosable loop.

## Slot 3 — The Format (how it's made)

| Code | Format | Build cost | Best funnel | Notes for this buyer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UGC** | Raw creator to camera | Low | ToF/MoF | The workhorse. 55–65 creator, sunlit kitchen. |
| **VSSL** | Visual-sound-script-loop, educational | Medium | ToF | 45–90s. Use only after a 20s hook has proven itself. |
| **DEMO** | Product demo / mechanism | Low | MoF | Show the capsule, the label, the dose. |
| **PROOF** | Extreme "prove it" — lab certificate, purity test on camera | Medium | MoF | Highest trust-conversion lever you have. |
| **AUTH** | Credible explainer — doctor, pharmacist, scientist, founder | Medium | MoF | Must be a genuine credential. Never fake a white coat. |
| **STREET** | Street interview with women 55–65 | High | ToF | Excellent, expensive. Batch 8 in one day. |
| **COMPARE** | Us vs. them / visual multiples | Low | MoF/BoF | 1 bottle vs. 6. Instant processing. |
| **STATIC** | High-contrast image | Very low | BoF | See `06-static-ads-sop.md`. Do not neglect these — she's a slow, careful reader. |
| **REVIEW** | Customer review compilation | Low | BoF | Screenshot reviews with a voiceover reading them. |
| **FOUNDER** | Founder to camera, why we made this | Low | ToF | Powerful against scam scepticism. |
| **MASHUP** | Best-performing clips recombined | Very low | MoF | Free creative from assets you already have. |

### The format ratio that works

For a 12-concept month:

```
5 × UGC/STREET  (ToF — the top of the funnel is where volume must be)
4 × PROOF/AUTH/DEMO/COMPARE  (MoF — where sceptics convert)
3 × STATIC/REVIEW  (BoF — where the bundle math closes)
```

Beginners over-index on bottom-of-funnel offer graphics because they're easy to make. That's why their accounts stop scaling: nothing is filling the top.

## The naming convention (this is how you get measurement)

You cannot learn from ads you can't sort. Name every asset:

```
[YYMM]_[FUNNEL]_[AVATAR]_[ANGLE]_[FORMAT]_[HOOKID]_[RATIO]_v[N]
```

**Example:** `2608_ToF_LIN_3PMCRASH_UGC_H02_9x16_v1`

| Token | Values |
|---|---|
| YYMM | 2608 = Aug 2026 |
| FUNNEL | ToF / MoF / BoF |
| AVATAR | LIN (Linda) / PRO (perimenopause professional) / ROB (Robert) |
| ANGLE | A1–A12 shorthand: WAKE, 3PMCRASH, GRANDKIDS, FOG, NAD, LABTEST, TRIAL, ONEVSIX, DOCTOR, CENTSDAY, GUARANTEE, FATHER |
| FORMAT | UGC / VSSL / DEMO / PROOF / AUTH / STREET / COMPARE / STATIC / REVIEW / FOUNDER / MASHUP |
| HOOKID | H01–H60, matching the hook library |
| RATIO | 9x16 / 4x5 / 1x1 |
| vN | v1, v2, v3 — variants of the same concept |

Use this name for: the source file, the ad name in Ads Manager, and the row in `templates/creative-tracker.csv`. When you later ask "do confession hooks beat listicle hooks for Linda?", the answer is a pivot table away instead of a guess.

## Concept quality gate

Before a concept goes into production, it must pass all six:

- [ ] I can name the **exact person** and the **exact moment** this is aimed at (including the hour of day).
- [ ] The first 3 seconds work with **sound off** *and* with **sound on** — both a visual and an audible hook.
- [ ] It pulls at least one of the **six psychological levers** from `01-foundation.md`.
- [ ] Every factual claim in it is on the **verified list** and I could produce the substantiation.
- [ ] It passes the **compliance rewrite check** in `11-compliance-guardrails.md`.
- [ ] It is **meaningfully different** from every other concept in this batch — not a colour change or a font swap.

If it fails any one, it isn't a concept yet. Fix it or drop it. Producing a weak concept costs you $350 of test budget you could have spent on a real one.

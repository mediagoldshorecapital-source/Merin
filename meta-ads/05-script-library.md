# 05 — Script Library: The Formula + 12 Production-Ready Scripts

---

## Part A — The formula

Every script is the same three blocks. Fill the blanks and you have a shootable ad.

```
[0:00–0:03]  HOOK
             Audible: ______________________________  (from 04-hook-library.md)
             Visual:  ______________________________  (must independently stop the scroll)
             OST:     ______________________________  (≤6 words, huge, high contrast)

[0:03–0:16]  BODY
             1. Relate    — "I felt exactly this" (2–3s)
             2. Mechanism — why it happens, in plain English (3–4s)
             3. Product   — name + dose + what changed, with a timeframe (4–5s)
             4. Proof     — lab test / review count / press badge on screen (2–3s)

[0:16–0:20]  CTA + EXPECTATIONS
             Action:      "Tap below…"
             Offer:       "Buy 3 Get 3 Free — $16.66 a bottle"
             Risk removal:"90-day money-back guarantee"
             Expectation: "Most people notice it around week three"
```

### The four non-negotiables in every script

1. **Product name, dose, and offer must be clear within the first 6 seconds** — not held back for a reveal. She isn't watching a film; she's deciding whether to keep watching.
2. **State a timeframe.** "It works" is unbelievable. "By week three" is believable. Always name when.
3. **Say what it *doesn't* do.** One honest limitation buys more trust than five benefits.
4. **The CTA names the bundle, the per-bottle price, and the guarantee.** Every time. She needs all three to act.

### Length guide

| Placement | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reels / Stories (9:16) | 15–25s | Default. Her evening scroll. |
| Feed (4:5) | 20–30s | She'll watch longer in Feed with sound on |
| Educational VSSL | 45–90s | Only after the 20s version proves the hook |
| Static | n/a | See `06-static-ads-sop.md` |
